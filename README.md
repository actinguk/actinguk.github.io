# Acting UK

An independent UK acting-career resource, built as a self-contained static website. No paid services, accounts, framework, build process or external asset requests are required.

## Contents
- 70 directory entries: 23 training, 15 representation, 11 casting, 13 support and 8 news sources.
- Eight original career guides and a dated industry briefing.
- Search, category and region filtering, sorting, pagination, a saved shortlist and career checklist (local browser storage).
- Original inline SVG editorial illustrations. No third-party photographs or logos are used.

## Research and product rationale
Spotlight (https://www.spotlight.com/industry-resources/) and Contacts (https://app.spotlight.com/contacts) provide professional casting and a broad industry directory. The Stage (https://www.thestage.co.uk/) specialises in UK theatre news and jobs. Backstage UK (https://www.backstage.com/uk/) and Mandy (https://www.mandy.com/) offer casting and advice. Equity (https://www.equity.org.uk/advice-and-support/casting-and-auditions) provides authoritative safety and rights guidance. The Federation of Drama Schools (https://www.federationofdramaschools.co.uk/) and CDMT (https://cdmt.org.uk/accredited-professional-training/) support research into training. The PMA (https://thepma.com/about-us/) and Agent.co.uk (https://agent.co.uk/acting-agents/) provide representation information. Casting Callback (https://castingcallback.com/) offers casting applications and actor tools. Oxford Careers (https://www.ox.ac.uk/careers/careers-guidance/explore-careers/sectors-and-occupations/performing-arts) and the National Careers Service (https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/job-profiles/actor) inform career routes.

Our distinction is a free-to-browse editorial hub that connects these sources across career stages instead of copying casting listings or claiming to be an agent. The directory is selective, not a ranking or endorsement. Each listing links to the organisation or resource, and users should verify current submission policies, eligibility and costs on the original source. News briefings are dated snapshots, not a live feed. Research compilation: 13 November 2026.

## Run or publish
Open index.html in a browser. To publish with GitHub Pages, open Settings → Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, select main and /(root), then Save. The standard project URL is https://actinguk.github.io/ once Pages is enabled and deployment succeeds.

## Maintenance
Edit the data and guides arrays in index.html. Keep links, course details and agency policies current; update the briefing date when replacing stories. Do not republish third-party articles or protected casting breakdowns. The site does not collect personal information or send data to a backend.

## Editorial voice and promotion
Acting UK speaks as a publication in a collective editorial voice. Use “we”, “our” and “we would recommend” for opinions and recommendations; never write in a first-person singular voice. Address readers as “you”, or use neutral question headings. Apply this consistently to article copy, question titles, related-reading links, metadata and checklist labels. Keep promotional recommendations understated and relevant. Do not name or link to competing headshot photographers or studios in customer-facing content unless the publisher explicitly requests it. Use independent industry and official sources for factual guidance.

## Private acting coaches
The separate coaches.html directory contains 39 named individuals and 39 static profile pages in coaches/. Profiles are grouped by primary specialism and filterable by name and technique. Reviews are editorial assessments of published services, not firsthand reviews or rankings. No pricing comparisons, direct booking links or external coach links are used. Confirm session availability with the provider. The directory is linked from the homepage and main resource directory; all new pages are in the sitemap.
