About Acting UK

An independent, free-to-browse editorial guide for actors in the British acting industry.

Why Acting UK exists

Acting UK brings together practical career guidance, training, casting and representation resources for actors at different stages. The directory is selective and is not an official accreditation or ranking.

Our editorial approach

Our articles are editorial resources, not advice from a casting director, lawyer or immigration adviser unless explicitly attributed to a qualified contributor.

Our standards

Read our editorial policy, directory methodology and author information. We distinguish published information from first-hand experience and link to original sources when available.
