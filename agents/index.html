UK acting agents and talent agencies

Find UK acting agencies and representation resources, and learn how to research and approach the right agent.

How to choose representation

Look for an agency whose roster and work align with your skills and career stage. Check its official submission instructions, whether it is accepting approaches, and which department handles actors. An agent can help with submissions and contracts, but representation is not a guarantee of work.

Before you apply

  • Prepare a current headshot, accurate CV and a short reel or scenes.
  • Read the agency’s submission rules and tailor your introduction.
  • Keep a record of dates and responses; respect closed books.
  • Review commission, exclusivity and termination terms before signing.
