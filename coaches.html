Private acting coaches in London.

— Find the right individual for the work you want to do. Explore 39 named coaches, from first auditions to professional role preparation.

Acting UK editorialReviewed 13 November 2026

Choose the coach, not the course.

Private tuition offers space to work on your particular strengths, habits and material. Whether you are preparing a self-tape, trying for drama school or rebuilding your craft, start with a clear objective and choose someone whose approach suits it.

Our directory focuses on named individuals rather than group-only schools. Some work through studios or academies; ask to confirm the person who will actually coach you. London availability varies, and some offer online sessions or in-person work by arrangement.

Before booking a private session

We would recommend agreeing on a concrete goal, such as preparing two contrasting speeches, rehearsing an audition scene or developing a repeatable technique. Ask who will coach you, where the work takes place, what material to prepare, whether a reader or recording is included, and what happens if you need to reschedule. For young performers, confirm safeguarding and parent or guardian arrangements. A private coach can support your development, but cannot guarantee representation, admission or a role.
