About the coach

Amy Tez’s professional coaching listing describes private actor coaching and performance intensives, including Meisner and audition work. Her current public website also emphasises leadership and communication coaching.

Our editorial review

We would consider her for actors who want to explore presence, confidence and bolder performance choices, but the current balance of her business makes it important to confirm that actor-specific sessions are available.

Before booking

Enquire explicitly about private acting rather than executive coaching, and agree a concrete performance objective.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
