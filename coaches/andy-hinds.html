About the coach

Andy Hinds is a theatre director, author and acting teacher whose published tuition information includes individual coaching for professional and student actors. His background includes teaching Shakespeare and text at major drama-training institutions.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would shortlist him for classical speeches, particularly when verse feels like a technical obstacle rather than a vehicle for action. His work with both professional and student actors makes him relevant to role preparation as well as entry auditions.

Before booking

Bring the full speech and its context in the play; ask whether the session will focus on verse, action or audition delivery.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
