About the coach

Ben Woodhall founded OnAction Studio and offers individual coaching for film, television and theatre. His training draws on Drama Centre approaches including Stanislavski, Meisner and character analysis, alongside a dedicated self-tape service.

Our editorial review

We would shortlist him when an actor needs creative direction and the practical production of a tape in the same workflow. This may be particularly useful for experienced performers balancing a tight deadline with the need to explore a scene.

Before booking

Confirm whether the session is coaching only or includes filming, editing and delivery; request Ben specifically if that is your preference.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
