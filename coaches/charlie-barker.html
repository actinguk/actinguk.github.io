About the coach

Charlie Barker’s Persever Productions page advertises individual tutoring for film and theatre auditions, self-tapes and drama-school applications. The current private offer is explicitly online; the page describes in-person workshops separately.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider her for applicants or actors who want detailed rehearsal and feedback on audition material without needing to travel. We would not treat a London in-person private session as confirmed from the published information.

Before booking

Ask whether face-to-face private work is currently offered or whether the session will be online.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
