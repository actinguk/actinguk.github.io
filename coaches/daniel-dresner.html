About the coach

Daniel Dresner’s coaching site advertises one-to-one acting and audition coaching, career development and on-set support. He describes working with actors in London and internationally, with in-person and online arrangements.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider him for performers seeking a combination of practical acting feedback and a clearer development plan. The distinction between craft and broader career coaching can help a client choose the right service for their immediate need.

Before booking

Ask for an actor-specific session and agree whether the focus is scene work, audition preparation or longer-term development.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
