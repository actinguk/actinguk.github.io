About the coach

Daniel O’Meara is an actor and founder of Actors Crib. Its published private tuition covers audition preparation, scene analysis, character work and emotional preparation, drawing on multiple acting traditions.

Our editorial review

We would consider him for actors who want a practical, flexible approach rather than adherence to one method. His individual sessions could be useful when a particular scene reveals a recurring difficulty with choices, focus or character.

Before booking

Agree whether the session is online or in person, and bring a scene that illustrates the skill you want to develop.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
