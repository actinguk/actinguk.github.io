About the coach

Danny Mahoney is a LAMDA-trained actor who advertises private coaching for professional auditions, self-tapes, drama-school entry and LAMDA examinations. His published biography describes screen and West End work.

Our editorial review

We would consider him for actors looking for practical audition guidance from someone actively familiar with professional performance environments. His range is useful for students who need to distinguish exam material from professional casting material.

Before booking

Clarify the audition or examination format and ask how feedback will translate into rehearsals you can repeat independently.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
