About the coach

Dominic Grant’s coaching listing describes private tuition and an initial assessment leading to a personal action plan. His professional background includes acting, directing and teaching at Morley College, where he has served as Head of Drama.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider him for adult learners who want to identify development priorities and practise them in a structured way. His teaching background may be particularly relevant for someone who wants continuity and concrete feedback.

Before booking

Ask what the initial assessment covers and whether a series of private sessions can be adapted to your experience.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
