About the coach

Edward Hicks’s private tuition page describes experience coaching actors and directors before and during film and television productions, alongside senior drama-training work. It explicitly describes his London private availability as occasional.

Our editorial review

We would consider him for a defined professional role or production challenge rather than assuming a regular weekly teaching slot. His background may be valuable when a performer needs to understand camera demands and the director’s expectations.

Before booking

Enquire about current availability and whether the work is appropriate to the role, project and experience level.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
