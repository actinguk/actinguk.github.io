About the coach

Eleanor Boyce offers individual voice and dialect coaching for auditions and productions. Her published services include accent development, speech, vocal delivery and on-set or ADR work across screen and theatre.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would shortlist her when a role demands an unfamiliar accent or greater vocal consistency. A specialist dialect session can help integrate sounds into character rather than simply imitating an accent mechanically.

Before booking

Send the audition brief and reference accent, and ask what practice materials or recording feedback will be available.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
