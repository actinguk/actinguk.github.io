About the coach

Emma Crompton’s professional biography describes private and on-set performance coaching alongside commercial casting. Her background includes helping established performers and people from other disciplines prepare screen roles.

Our editorial review

We would consider her for actors transitioning into unfamiliar screen work or needing performance support around a production. Her casting experience is useful context, but coaching should not be understood as access to casting opportunities.

Before booking

Check whether she is taking private clients, the session location and the specific performance work she can support.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
