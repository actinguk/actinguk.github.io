About the coach

Emma King-Farlow is the artistic director of Shadow Road Productions and advertises private coaching in Barnes and online. Her services cover auditions, Shakespeare and general skill development.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider her for a performer seeking a director’s perspective and a supportive route into text work. The individual format can suit a beginner developing a speech or an actor wanting to refine a specific audition.

Before booking

Ask what material to bring and whether the session will include selection, rehearsal or a broader skills assessment.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
