About the coach

Grace Andrews advertises private actor tuition with monologue feedback, craft teaching, breath and text work. Her published arrangements allow online coaching and occasional London sessions; studio hire may be possible.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider her for applicants who have already chosen material and want a deeper understanding of the speech, as well as trained actors refreshing their practice. Her guidance on preparation makes it useful to arrive with well-learned text.

Before booking

Confirm London arrangements and bring a speech you can perform without relying on the page.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
