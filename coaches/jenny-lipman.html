About the coach

Jenny Lipman’s professional site describes more than 20 years as an acting tutor and director at LAMDA, alongside teaching at other leading drama schools. Her individual coaching specialises in drama-school auditions.

Our editorial review

We would shortlist her for applicants who need to understand their material, respond to direction and develop an audition repertoire. A tutor familiar with conservatoire training can help prepare the work without making a student imitate an expected audition style.

Before booking

Discuss the schools being targeted, their requirements and whether classical and contemporary pieces need to be selected or refined.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
