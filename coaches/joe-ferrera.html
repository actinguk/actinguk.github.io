About the coach

Joe Ferrera is an actor, writer and director whose studio advertises individual coaching. His professional listing includes screen acting, comedy, auditions and several acting traditions.

Our editorial review

We would consider him for performers who want to explore a wider range of choices and develop versatility across dramatic and comedic material. The mix of methods makes it worth discussing which approach is appropriate rather than trying to use every technique at once.
