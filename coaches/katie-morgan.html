About the coach

Katie Morgan founded The Actors’ Surgery, which advertises individual coaching, performance consultancy and career advice. Her professional biography describes training at East 15 and École Gaulier and teaching and coaching across theatre and screen.

Our editorial review

We would consider her for performers returning to acting or wanting to examine which parts of their technique need attention. The broad experience may suit an initial assessment followed by a focused programme of practice.

Before booking

Ask what the first session can diagnose and whether subsequent sessions can be planned around specific acting goals.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
