About the coach

Kieran Vyas is a director and acting coach whose published method combines motivation psychology with physical, vocal and imaginative work. His coaching page describes online sessions and limited face-to-face availability in London and Leicester.

Our editorial review

We would consider him for actors who understand a scene intellectually but struggle to make it feel immediate and alive. His attention to bodily freedom and motivation may help bridge analysis and spontaneous performance.

Before booking

Ask about the next London dates or arrange online work, and bring a scene with a clear dramatic problem.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
