About the coach

Home Studio London advertises private coaching alongside its workshops and longer courses, with Laurence Mitchell and guest tutors. The studio describes working with performers from beginners to experienced professionals.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider him for actors interested in a sustained practice environment rather than a one-off audition fix. The studio’s emphasis on training over time may be helpful when an actor wants to identify habits and work on them consistently.

Before booking

Confirm a private session with Laurence himself, rather than another tutor, and discuss a specific long-term goal.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
