About the coach

Lotte Ruth Johnson’s professional coaching listing describes theatre direction, an MA in Acting Coaching and individual psychophysical training. The listed work includes voice, movement, improvisation, text and audition preparation.

Our editorial review

We would consider her for actors who want to connect physical awareness with imaginative and emotional choices. Her broader development approach may be helpful where a performer needs a supportive space to explore habits without focusing solely on a forthcoming audition.

Before booking

Discuss the physical demands, accessibility needs and boundaries of the exercises, and agree what the session should address.
