About the coach

Mel Churcher combines acting, voice and presentation coaching with an extensive record of film and television production work. Her published biography describes more than 60 productions and private sessions alongside workshops.

Our editorial review

We would consider her particularly relevant for an actor who needs to connect vocal and physical freedom with the demands of a camera. An actor preparing a role can benefit from an approach that connects a scene’s emotional life to spoken dialogue rather than treating voice and performance as separate exercises.

Before booking

Bring a scene and ask how the work will address both performance choices and vocal habits; confirm her London availability.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
