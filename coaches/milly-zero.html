About the coach

Milly Zero is a BRIT School-trained working actor and founder of Here For The Drama. Its individual coaching page names her among the coaches offering private technique and audition support for children, teenagers and adults.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider her especially for younger performers and emerging screen actors who want practical support with confidence and taped auditions. Parents should confirm safeguarding arrangements and the session format before booking.

Before booking

Request Milly specifically, confirm the location and ask whether the lesson is coaching only or includes filming.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
