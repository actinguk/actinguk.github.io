About the coach

Munir Khairdin offers private acting lessons, audition preparation and production support. His published services cover screen and stage technique, voice, text, character work and practical self-tape direction.

Our editorial review

We would consider him for an emerging actor who wants to build confidence in front of a camera while keeping the work connected to the script. The range also makes sense for working performers preparing a specific role or audition.

Before booking

Bring the audition brief or scene and ask whether a recording and follow-up notes can be included.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
