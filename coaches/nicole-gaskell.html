About the coach

Nicole Gaskell advertises individual coaching rooted in Meisner technique, with an emphasis on truthful response, connection and presence. Her professional page describes London face-to-face and online coaching.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider her for actors whose performances feel planned or self-conscious, and who want to practise listening and responding more freely. Ask how individual sessions adapt a technique often developed through interaction with a scene partner.

Before booking

Discuss the balance of one-to-one exercises, scene work and any partner practice before booking.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
