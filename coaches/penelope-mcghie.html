About the coach

Penelope McGhie’s professional coaching listing describes extensive acting experience and an MA in Actor Training and Coaching from Royal Central. It lists private tuition, with a focus on drama-school preparation, text and physical embodiment.

Our editorial review

We would consider her for applicants who want a collaborative method that goes beyond delivering a memorised speech. The combination of analysis and physical exploration may help an actor discover more playable, less rehearsed choices.

Before booking

Ask about the balance of text, movement and audition practice, and discuss any accessibility requirements.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
