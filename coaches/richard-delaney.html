About the coach

Richard Delaney offers individual coaching, role preparation and production support, drawing on Michael Chekhov, Stanislavski, Viewpoints and his own practice. His published biography also describes actor-training work at Royal Central.

Our editorial review

We would consider him for trained actors wanting to develop a more flexible physical and imaginative process, particularly when existing habits limit the range of a character. The approach is suited to a specific rehearsal challenge or longer craft development.

Before booking

Bring a scene and ask which physical or imaginative exercises will be appropriate to your needs.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
