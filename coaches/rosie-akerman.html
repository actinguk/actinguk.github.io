About the coach

Rosie Akerman is an actor and director associated with N1 Self Tapes, which advertises private audition preparation and a recording service in Old Street. The service aims to let performers focus on their scene instead of the technical setup.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider her especially for a current taped audition where an actor needs a reader, direction and reliable recording conditions. It is a focused practical service rather than a replacement for a broad acting-training programme.

Before booking

Ask what is included in coaching versus recording, the delivery arrangements and whether an additional rehearsal is useful.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
