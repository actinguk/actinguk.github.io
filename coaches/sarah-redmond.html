About the coach

Sarah Redmond describes private work as an acting coach and dramaturge, supporting character development, script analysis, drama-school applications and professional auditions. Her work includes film and television audition coaching.

Our editorial review

We would consider her for actors who want a director’s perspective on what a scene is doing, rather than only technical delivery. This may be especially helpful when a performance needs clearer intentions or a stronger sense of the world of the play.

Before booking

Send the scene or audition instructions and agree whether the focus is text interpretation, performance or both.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
