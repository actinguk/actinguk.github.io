About the coach

Simon Furness’s professional coaching listing describes teaching Meisner at the Actors Temple and subsequently Actors East, alongside private tuition in acting and audition work.

Our editorial review

We would consider him for actors who want to develop responsiveness and listening, especially if they feel their performance is too predetermined. Meisner-based practice can be particularly useful when scene work is paired with regular opportunities to interact with others.
