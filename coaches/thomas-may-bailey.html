About the coach

A director and private acting coach, Thomas May Bailey offers role rehearsals, audition coaching, self-tape preparation and technique development. His Deep Scene Work approach connects the structure of a scene with character psychology and practical rehearsal.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would shortlist him for actors who enjoy close reading and want to turn analysis into playable action. The method appears especially useful where a scene has complicated relationships or shifts in power that need to be clear in performance.

Before booking

Bring a specific scene and ask whether rehearsal will include a recorded run or feedback on different choices.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
