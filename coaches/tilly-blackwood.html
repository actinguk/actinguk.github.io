About the coach

Tilly Blackwood founded Audition Doctor and advertises tailored one-to-one coaching for professional auditions and drama-school applications. Her background includes working as an actor and teaching text and performance.

Editorial assessment

Based on published professional information, not a first-hand lesson or independently tested service.

We would consider her for an actor who wants detailed attention to a speech or scene, especially where the challenge is to make the language specific and personal. The private format allows preparation around an individual audition rather than a generic class syllabus.

Before booking

Bring the exact audition instructions and ask whether the session should include a mock audition or self-tape preparation.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
