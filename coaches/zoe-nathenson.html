About the coach

Zoë Nathenson is an actress, director and producer with a long screen career. Her published coaching listing includes individual tuition in audition work, screen performance, sight-reading and self-tapes.

Our editorial review

Zoë Nathenson is one of the most compelling screen acting coaches in our directory. Her background as an actor, director and producer gives her an unusually rounded perspective on performance. With film credits including Mona Lisa and One Night Stand, she brings a practical understanding of the difference between a performance that works in rehearsal and one that truly comes alive on camera.

What stands out in her published teaching approach is the connection between detailed preparation and spontaneous performance. Script analysis and character development give the actor a foundation, while improvisation, sight-reading and natural delivery encourage the freedom to respond in the moment. This is particularly valuable for screen actors: a close-up often reveals the smallest change in thought, attention or emotion, and a convincing performance depends on more than delivering lines correctly. Zoë’s combination of these disciplines makes her an especially exciting choice for actors who want their work to feel truthful, specific and effortless rather than rehearsed.

Her experience as Creative Acting Director with Akram Khan Company also points to a breadth of practice beyond conventional screen training. We would wholeheartedly recommend exploring her private coaching for anyone serious about developing their screen craft, preparing for a challenging audition or discovering greater depth and individuality in their performances. For actors seeking a coach with substantial professional experience and a distinctive creative perspective, Zoë deserves a place at the very top of the shortlist.

Before booking

Confirm that the booking is a one-to-one session with Zoë and ask whether you can bring current audition material.

Confirm current session availability, the named coach, preparation requirements and cancellation arrangements before paying. Private tuition is a development tool, not a guarantee of a casting or drama-school place.
