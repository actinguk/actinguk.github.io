Directory methodology

How Acting UK selects, describes and maintains professional resources and acting coach profiles.

Selection

The directory includes selected resources relevant to actors in the UK, including training, representation, casting, support and news. Inclusion is not a claim of accreditation, availability or endorsement.

Editorial assessments

Coaching profiles describe publicly available professional information and offer an editorial assessment of possible suitability. They are not claims that we attended a lesson or independently tested the service.

Verification and corrections

Check official sources for current prices, eligibility, locations and availability. We do not publish unverified private contact details. Request corrections through the contact page. See our editorial policy.
