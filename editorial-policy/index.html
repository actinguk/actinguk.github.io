Editorial policy

How Acting UK researches, presents and corrects information about the UK acting industry.

Research and verification

We use original provider information, official industry bodies and government guidance where relevant. We distinguish factual claims, editorial interpretation and practical suggestions. Time-sensitive information should be checked against the original source before acting on it.

Independence and conflicts

Inclusion is not an endorsement or ranking. Editorial assessments based on public information are not first-hand reviews of services.

Corrections and updates

We aim to correct material errors and update changed guidance. Contact information and submission details can change; use the original provider’s instructions. See contact and corrections.
