A 30-day actor action plan

Turn a vague ambition into a small, repeatable system.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Five practical steps

  1. Week 1: audit your CV, headshot, reel and current training. Choose one specific goal and identify what is missing.
  2. Week 2: research ten appropriate organisations, agents or training providers. Identify the best matches and check the official application requirements.
  3. Week 3: practise a new scene, record a self-tape and send a small number of tailored applications. Log your submissions and follow-up dates.
  4. Week 4: review what worked, update your materials and schedule your next training or creative project.
  5. Repeat monthly. Measure actions you control, not only auditions or bookings.

Measure your progress

Track completed training, new scenes, verified applications and feedback. At the end of each week, record what you learned and one action to repeat. Avoid treating bookings as the only measure of progress.
