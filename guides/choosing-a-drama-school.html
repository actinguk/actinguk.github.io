Choosing a drama school

Find the training that fits your life, not just the famous name.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Five practical steps

  1. Compare degree, foundation, part-time and short-course routes. A degree is a major time and financial commitment; a foundation or weekly class can help you test your goals.
  2. Check actual course content, teaching staff, screen training, voice and movement, access needs, performance opportunities and graduate support.
  3. Budget for tuition, living costs, travel and audition fees. Check student finance, scholarships and Dance and Drama Awards directly with providers.
  4. Prepare contrasting monologues, read each play and understand the character’s objective. Follow the school’s audition instructions exactly.
  5. Speak with current students if possible. Compare offers by fit, costs and support rather than prestige alone.
