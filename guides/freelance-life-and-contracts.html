Freelance life and contracts

Understand the business side before a job becomes a problem.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Five practical steps

  1. Keep records of income, expenses, invoices and contracts. Check HMRC guidance on your own tax obligations; employment status can differ by engagement.
  2. Read the contract before accepting: dates, pay, travel, overtime, cancellation, usage, exclusivity and credit can matter as much as the headline fee.
  3. For commercials and digital work, ask how long and where your likeness may be used, including renewals and any AI or synthetic performance terms.
  4. Compare the applicable Equity agreements or other relevant negotiated rates rather than assuming one rate covers every production.
  5. Maintain a cash buffer where possible and plan for gaps between jobs. Seek qualified advice for tax, legal or contractual issues.
