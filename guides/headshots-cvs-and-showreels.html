Headshots, CVs and showreels

Build a professional package that makes casting decisions easier.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Five practical steps

  1. Choose a current, recognisable headshot that reflects your appearance and feels appropriate for the roles you want. Avoid extreme retouching or costume-heavy images.
  2. Keep your CV clean: contact or representation details, training, credits, relevant skills and accurate playing information. Use consistent names across profiles.
  3. Lead your reel with strong acting, not a long montage. Select short scenes that make your face, voice and choices easy to understand.
  4. If you do not have credits, create a well-rehearsed scene with a reader or collaborator. Label it accurately rather than presenting it as a released production.
  5. Keep materials updated after significant changes in appearance, training or footage. Ask for specific feedback instead of collecting endless new versions.
