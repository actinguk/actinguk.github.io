How to get an acting agent

Make a targeted approach rather than sending a hundred identical emails.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Five practical steps

  1. Choose agents whose existing roster and work align with your ambitions. Check whether they represent actors at your career stage and where they operate.
  2. Prepare a concise introduction, CV, current headshot and showreel or short scenes. Link to your professional profile if available.
  3. Follow each agency’s official submission route. If books are closed, respect that. Personalise why you are contacting them and avoid large unsolicited attachments.
  4. At a meeting, ask about commission, exclusivity, expenses, payments, termination and how the agency communicates with clients.
  5. Read the agreement before signing. Ask Equity for advice if unsure, and be cautious about pressure, guaranteed jobs or expensive mandatory packages.
