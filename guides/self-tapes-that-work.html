Self-tapes that work

Simple production choices that let your performance lead.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Five practical steps

  1. Read the casting brief and deliver the framing, file names, orientation and deadline requested. Ask for clarification when essential information is missing.
  2. Choose a quiet room, plain background and soft light on your face. Stabilise your camera at eye level and test sound before recording.
  3. Use an off-camera reader where requested. Keep the eyeline close to the lens unless the brief says otherwise. Avoid distracting editing or music.
  4. Find a clear objective, listen and respond. Record a few thoughtful takes rather than chasing technical perfection.
  5. Review the final file, check the opening and end, and upload securely using the requested method. Confirm that the link works before sending.
