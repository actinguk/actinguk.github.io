Spot a casting scam

Protect your money, personal information and boundaries.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Five practical steps

  1. Confirm the production, casting professional and contact details independently. A social account or convincing email is not proof of legitimacy.
  2. Be cautious about requests to pay for an audition, urgent transfers, unusual payment arrangements or guarantees of employment. Check official guidance.
  3. Ask for role details, dates, location, pay, usage and any intimacy or physical requirements before agreeing. Do not send sensitive documents without a legitimate need.
  4. For an unfamiliar in-person audition, tell someone where you are going and check the venue. You can request accessible arrangements or raise concerns about boundaries.
  5. If something feels wrong, stop and seek advice. Equity provides confidential guidance on casting and auditions.

Check the opportunity

Confirm the production identity, location, pay, usage and casting contact through independent sources. A vague offer, pressure to pay immediately or requests for sensitive material should prompt further checks. Do not send private documents unnecessarily.

Read UK casting resources and consult Equity for current safety guidance.
