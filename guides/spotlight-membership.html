Spotlight membership: eligibility, credits and UK fees

Understand Spotlight's adult performer joining routes, qualifying professional credits, training and recommendations, with published UK membership prices.

How do you qualify for Spotlight? Adult performers can apply through professional performance experience, recognised professional training or an eligible professional recommendation. These are alternatives, not three requirements that everyone must satisfy. Spotlight makes the eligibility decision; always consult its official joining guidance before applying.

The three routes for adult performers

Professional performance experience

Spotlight's published criteria allow one featured performance credit under an Equity or equivalent union contract, or two contracted professional paid performance credits. Keep evidence of the production, role, contract and payment. Your experience must meet the current definitions; simply having two entries on a CV does not guarantee acceptance.

Professional training

The training route includes at least one year of full-time professional performance training equivalent to RQF Level 5, or two years of eligible part-time professional performance training. Ask Spotlight if you are unsure about the level or format of a course. A weekend workshop or short introductory course should not be assumed to qualify.

Professional recommendation

A qualifying recommendation can be another route. It must come from an eligible professional who has the required professional connection with you, such as a Spotlight-registered agent or another eligible professional under Spotlight's rules. A casual endorsement, paid reference or recommendation from a friend is not equivalent. Consult Spotlight's current eligibility guidance and contact the membership team if unclear.

Which credits do not normally qualify?

Spotlight distinguishes professional performance credits from supporting artiste or extra work, body doubling, stunt work, amateur and unpaid productions, and productions completed as part of a course. Do not rely on those categories for the professional-credit route. Self-produced work can require evidence of financial or contractual gain and a paying audience; international credits may be considered with appropriate evidence. Check Spotlight's definitions for your specific production.

Can you join without an agent?

Eligible adult performers can join as self-represented performers. You do not have to secure representation first. Young performer membership has separate rules, including representation by a Spotlight-registered agent. See Spotlight's membership FAQs.

UK membership prices

Published prices checked 13 November 2026 on Spotlight's performer membership page:

  • Adult performer: £210 per year including VAT, or £19.20 per month on the monthly payment option.
  • Graduate and young performer: £136.50 per year or £12.48 per month, subject to the applicable membership rules.

Twelve adult monthly payments total £230.40, compared with £210 annually. Check the payment terms, eligibility for discounts and any bursary before subscribing. These fees can change. See also Spotlight's membership pricing help.

Prepare before you apply

Gather your headshot, CV, details of professional training and supporting evidence for credits or recommendation. Make sure your CV describes your actual role and experience accurately. Read the application form carefully and contact Spotlight about borderline credits instead of claiming a category you cannot substantiate.

Once accepted, keep your profile current, use appropriate skills and playing-age information, and upload suitable footage where available. Membership provides access to a casting platform; it is not a guarantee of auditions, work or representation.

If you are not eligible yet

Do not spend money simply to manufacture credits. Build your craft, seek genuine opportunities and retain evidence of your work. Our route without drama school, career-starting budget and casting hub offer practical next steps.
