Actor headshots in the UK

Practical guidance on choosing, preparing for and using professional actor headshots in the UK.

What a casting headshot needs to do

A useful acting headshot is current, recognisable and appropriate for the work you seek. It should help a casting professional understand how you might appear on screen or stage rather than disguise your appearance.

Plan your materials

  • Compare complete session prices and the number of finished photographs.
  • Choose clothing and styling that support your casting range.
  • Ask about retouching, usage rights and delivery before booking.
  • Keep your headshots consistent with your CV and showreel.
