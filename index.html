06 / About this project

Independent by design.

Acting UK is a free starting point for actors at every career stage, across the UK.

We curate links to original sources and write our own guides. Our directory is selective, not exhaustive. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, affiliation or accreditation. We do not sell casting access, promise representation or collect application details. Always use the provider’s own instructions and verify any casting notice before applying.

Research references: University of Oxford careers · Spotlight · Federation of Drama Schools · PMA · Equity. Editorial compilation: 13 November 2026.
