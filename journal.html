02 / The questions

Real questions. Useful answers.

These are editorial questions inspired by recurring public actor discussions and professional guidance, not invented interviews or testimonials. Search by subject or choose a theme. Each question opens a dedicated article with its own permanent address.

24 questions
01Auditions

How do you get your first acting audition in the UK with no experience?

You do not have to wait for an agent to start. Build a small, credible portfolio and look for opportunities that match your present skills.↗
02Auditions

Do you need Spotlight or an agent to get auditions?

No. But a professional casting profile and good representation can open doors that are harder to reach alone.↗
03Headshots

Where should you get your first professional acting headshots in London?

Begin with a photograph that looks like you, not a glamorous version of someone else. A strong headshot should make casting want to see the actor behind it.↗
04Auditions

Why are you applying for roles but never getting auditions?

Silence is common, but it is useful to separate the things you can change from the things you cannot.↗
05Auditions

How do you get a London acting agent if you have no connections?

Look for a professional fit, not the biggest name. The best first agency is one that understands your work and has room for you.↗
06Auditions

Do you need an expensive self-tape setup?

No. Clear sound, useful framing and a good performance are the priorities.↗
07Auditions

What if a student film never sends you your showreel footage?

Ask for a clear delivery arrangement before you commit. A promise of footage is not the same as a delivery date.↗
08London life

Is it still worth moving to London to become an actor?

London is useful, not compulsory. The right question is whether living there gives you enough opportunity to justify its cost.↗
09London life

How much money do you really need to rent and live in London as an actor?

Budget for the room you can actually rent, not an average for the whole city.↗
10London life

Where should actors live in London?

Choose a room around your work, transport and daily life, rather than the prestige of a postcode.↗
11London life

How can a freelance actor rent a flat without a regular salary?

Prepare evidence of affordability before you view. A good application is clear, honest and easy to check.↗
12Money & career

What is the best survival job for an actor in London?

The best job pays reliably and leaves enough energy and flexibility for acting.↗
13Money & career

Should you take unpaid or very low-paid acting work for the experience?

A useful credit can be valuable, but experience does not automatically make an exploitative arrangement acceptable.↗
14Money & career

Is drama school really worth the money?

It can be transformative, but the name of a school is not a guarantee of representation or paid work.↗
15Money & career

Are you too old to start acting at 30, 40 or 50?

There is no universal expiry date on starting. Your training route and practical goals matter more than a deadline you invent for yourself.↗
16International

Can you work as an actor in the UK on a Student visa?

Do not assume so. The Student route has specific restrictions that are especially important for drama students.↗
17International

Is studying acting in London worth it for an international student?

It can be, if the education is valuable on its own and you have a realistic immigration and financial plan.↗
18International

Can you stay in the UK and act after graduating from drama school?

Possibly. Your course, provider, existing permission and application timing matter.↗
19International

Can you get a UK acting agent while living abroad?

You can research and approach agencies from abroad, but being represented is not the same as having permission to work.↗
20AI & rights

Is AI going to replace actors? Should you still pursue acting?

Nobody can reliably predict how many roles AI will change or displace. Prepare for the uncertainty rather than accepting either panic or reassurance as fact.↗
21AI & rights

What should you do if a production asks to scan your face or clone your voice?

Ask what the scan is for, how it will be used, and what you are being paid to agree to before signing.↗
22AI & rights

Can you use AI to prepare auditions or improve your headshots?

Use AI as a private assistant for low-risk preparation, not as a substitute for your actual performance or identity.↗
23Headshots

How many acting headshots do you need, and how often should you update them?

You need a small, coherent set that communicates your range without confusing casting about what you look like.↗
24Auditions

How do you know whether a casting call is a scam?

A real-looking website or social profile is not enough. Verify the people, production and terms independently.↗

03 / How we work

Research, not rumours.

The questions were developed from public conversations in r/ActingUK and r/acting, including discussions about moving to London, survival income, representation and getting footage. Community experiences are anecdotal, not industry statistics. Answers use official guidance from GOV.UK, Equity, Spotlight, ONS, Shelter and professional industry bodies, together with original editorial judgement. Sources are attached to each answer. Legal, immigration and rental information is general information for readers to check against their own circumstances; rules change. No user stories are fabricated or presented as interviews.

Acting UK is published by Kirill Kozlov, who also runs Kirill Kozlov Photography. All Journal resources are free to read, with no purchase required. The site does not collect questions or account information; this is an editorial Q&A, not an interactive advice service.
