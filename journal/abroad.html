Can you get a UK acting agent while living abroad?

— You can research and approach agencies from abroad, but being represented is not the same as having permission to work.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Explain your actual location, citizenship or work-permission position, languages, accents and ability to travel. Do not say that you are London-based or have unrestricted work rights if that is not true.

The Creative Worker route requires a qualifying engagement and a certificate from a licensed sponsor. It is not a general permission to relocate and look for work. Other immigration routes may be relevant depending on your circumstances.

An agency may be interested in a distinctive skill or casting profile, but last-minute recalls and travel costs can make some work impractical. Seek advice about permitted visitor activities and work before accepting engagements or travelling.
