Is AI going to replace actors? Should you still pursue acting?

— Nobody can reliably predict how many roles AI will change or displace. Prepare for the uncertainty rather than accepting either panic or reassurance as fact.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Synthetic voices, digital replicas and generated video already raise questions about consent, compensation and future work. Different sectors may be affected differently; commercial, voice and background work are not identical to live theatre or character performance.

Invest in skills that remain useful across formats: listening, interpretation, voice, movement, improvisation, collaboration and the ability to take direction. Learn how digital production works without handing over control of your identity.

Read Equity's AI guidance and check the terms of every recording, scan or voice session. Track the industry agreements that apply to your work. Our view is that a career plan should combine craft, financial resilience and contract literacy, not a prediction that AI will either destroy or save the profession.
