Can you use AI to prepare auditions or improve your headshots?

— Use AI as a private assistant for low-risk preparation, not as a substitute for your actual performance or identity.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

You might use AI to organise a rehearsal schedule, suggest questions about a character or practise an interview. Check its output; it can invent context and industry rules. Do not upload confidential casting sides, contracts, other performers' recordings or unreleased scripts without permission.

For headshots, a heavily generated portrait may create a casting mismatch. A current photograph that shows your actual appearance is the safer professional choice. Routine colour correction and restrained retouching are different from replacing facial features or inventing a photograph.

Always follow the casting brief and confidentiality requirements. If a submission uses synthetic audio, video or imagery, check whether that is acceptable and disclose it where required. Never pass synthetic work off as a real credit.
