What should you do if a production asks to scan your face or clone your voice?

— Ask what the scan is for, how it will be used, and what you are being paid to agree to before signing.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Request a clear written description of the intended production, permitted uses, territory, duration, storage, access, sublicensing, model training and any future reuse. Ask whether a replica can create new performances without you and whether you can refuse unrelated uses.

Check whether there is separate informed consent, how compensation works for the initial scan and later uses, and what happens on expiry or termination. Keep a copy of the contract and any consent form.

Equity's position is that a historic performance contract for a particular production does not, by itself, provide a lawful basis for AI training or replicas. This is a legal and contractual issue with context-specific exceptions and developments; get union or specialist advice before agreeing.
