Is drama school really worth the money?

— It can be transformative, but the name of a school is not a guarantee of representation or paid work.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Evaluate the actual course: teaching hours, class size, voice and movement, screen training, access support, productions, showcase and graduate outcomes. Ask what happens if you need to defer, withdraw or repeat a year.

Work out tuition, accommodation, living costs, travel, audition fees and lost earnings. Check funding and course eligibility directly with the school. Compare a three-year course with a foundation year or part-time route plus regular practice.

Ask current students and graduates about the training itself, not just the best-known alumni. A good decision should leave you with stronger skills and a sustainable plan, even if an agent does not sign you immediately.
