How do you get your first acting audition in the UK with no experience?

— You do not have to wait for an agent to start. Build a small, credible portfolio and look for opportunities that match your present skills.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Start by taking a class or joining a local theatre or filmmaking group. Record two contrasting short scenes, create a truthful CV and take a clear, recent headshot. A phone, a neutral background and good light are enough to begin.

Look at open calls from established theatre companies, verified casting platforms and film-school projects. Read the entire brief before applying: role, location, pay, dates and eligibility. Submit specifically for roles you can actually do, rather than every role you see.

Set a manageable weekly routine: practise one scene, identify suitable opportunities, send targeted applications and track replies. Do not pay someone simply to guarantee an audition. Our directory is a starting point, not a list of currently available jobs.
