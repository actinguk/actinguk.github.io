What if a student film never sends you your showreel footage?

— Ask for a clear delivery arrangement before you commit. A promise of footage is not the same as a delivery date.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Before a shoot, agree in writing what footage you may use, when you can expect it, and whether festival or distribution restrictions apply. Keep the producer's contact details and the agreement.

If the film is delayed, request a short update and ask whether a private scene extract can be released sooner. Be reasonable about post-production timelines but set a follow-up date.

If nothing arrives, make a strong scene with a collaborator rather than waiting indefinitely. Label it accurately as self-produced material. Do not use copyrighted footage without the necessary permission.
