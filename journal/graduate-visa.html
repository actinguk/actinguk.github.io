Can you stay in the UK and act after graduating from drama school?

— Possibly. Your course, provider, existing permission and application timing matter.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

The Graduate route is available to eligible graduates who meet its requirements; it is not available simply because someone completed any acting course. For eligible non-doctoral applicants, the rules currently provide two years for applications made before 1 January 2027 and 18 months for applications on or after that date. Eligible doctoral graduates have a three-year grant.

Graduate permission permits work including self-employment, except as a professional sportsperson, and has no access to public funds. It is time-limited and is not itself a settlement route. Do not make a career plan assuming it can be extended indefinitely.

If you want to continue after graduation, explore the appropriate immigration routes early, including sponsored Creative Worker engagements where eligible. Obtain individual advice before paying for a course or accepting a job.
