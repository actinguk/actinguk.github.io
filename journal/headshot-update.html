How many acting headshots do you need, and how often should you update them?

— You need a small, coherent set that communicates your range without confusing casting about what you look like.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Start with one clear lead image and a few useful alternatives that show meaningful differences in expression, light or styling. A commercial, approachable image and a quieter dramatic image can be useful, but choose according to the work you pursue.

Update when your appearance changes substantially, when your materials no longer represent your casting range or when trusted industry feedback identifies a specific gap. There is no universal annual rule. Do not spend money just to follow a trend.

Before a session, think about the roles you might realistically play and bring clothes that support those directions without becoming costumes. A photographer who works regularly with actors can help you find variety while keeping all the photographs authentically you.
