Is studying acting in London worth it for an international student?

— It can be, if the education is valuable on its own and you have a realistic immigration and financial plan.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Check whether the course provider is a licensed Student sponsor, whether the course qualifies for the visa you need and whether it can lead to a Graduate visa. Not every acting course or drama school programme does. Ask the provider for written confirmation before paying a deposit.

As of 13 November 2026, the Student visa maintenance requirement for London is £1,529 per month for up to nine months, where the requirement applies, in addition to relevant course fees. That is a visa evidence requirement, not a guarantee of sufficient living costs. Budget separately for a full year, visa fees, immigration health surcharge, travel and currency risk.

Compare the course with options in your home country and outside London. Consider the opportunity to build craft and relationships, how your visa affects work during study, and what you will do if you do not secure further permission or representation afterwards.
