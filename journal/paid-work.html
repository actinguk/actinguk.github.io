Should you take unpaid or very low-paid acting work for the experience?

— A useful credit can be valuable, but experience does not automatically make an exploitative arrangement acceptable.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Before saying yes, ask what you will learn, who is involved, what footage or credit you can use, and the total commitment. Calculate travel, accommodation, rehearsal time and lost day-job earnings. An apparently paid job can still cost you money.

Check the legal employment status and applicable minimum wage rules. A production calling a role a volunteer opportunity does not necessarily determine whether minimum wage is owed. Equity can advise on agreements and minimum rates.

Choose selectively: one well-organised project that develops your craft or gives you usable material may be worth more than several vague promises. Ask for written terms and decline if the financial or safety risk is too high.
