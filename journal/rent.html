How much money do you really need to rent and live in London as an actor?

— Budget for the room you can actually rent, not an average for the whole city.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Start with current room listings rather than whole-flat averages. As context, the ONS October 2026 release recorded an average UK private rent of £1,406 in September; that is not a London room price. Borough and property type make a huge difference.

Illustrative monthly budget, not a market quote: £900 room rent, £130 utilities and council tax contribution, £180 travel, £300 food and essentials, £100 acting costs and £150 buffer totals £1,760. Replace each line with your own quotes and include tax where relevant. Do not treat projected acting earnings as guaranteed income.

For moving in, budget for the first rent payment, the permitted tenancy deposit, moving costs and an emergency reserve. Check what bills are included, whether you have a quiet self-tape space and how long the commute takes at the times you actually travel.
