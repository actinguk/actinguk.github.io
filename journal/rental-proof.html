How can a freelance actor rent a flat without a regular salary?

— Prepare evidence of affordability before you view. A good application is clear, honest and easy to check.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Gather recent bank statements, invoices or tax records as appropriate, references and proof of identity and right to rent. Ask the agent what evidence they accept for self-employed applicants and whether a guarantor is an option.

In England, for covered private assured periodic tenancies, the rules changed on 1 May 2026. Landlords cannot request or accept rent before the agreement is signed; after signing they can generally request up to one month's rent in advance. A holding deposit is generally capped at one week's rent, and a tenancy deposit is normally capped at five weeks for annual rent below £50,000.

Do not hand over money to an unverified listing or send identity documents indiscriminately. Check the property's existence, the agent or landlord, the written terms and the deposit protection arrangements. Different rules can apply to lodgers and other accommodation types.
