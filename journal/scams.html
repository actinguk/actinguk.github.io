How do you know whether a casting call is a scam?

— A real-looking website or social profile is not enough. Verify the people, production and terms independently.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Check the name of the casting professional and company, official contact information, venue and production details. Ask for the role, dates, rate, usage and contract. Search for independent evidence that the organisation exists.

Be especially careful about payment to secure a role, pressure to act immediately, unexpected requests for identity documents, requests for intimate material, or private meetings without a credible explanation. Legitimate paid training is different from paying someone to guarantee a job.

If something feels unsafe, stop and seek advice. Keep evidence of suspicious messages and use Equity's casting guidance. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services.
