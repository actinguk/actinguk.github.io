Do you need an expensive self-tape setup?

— No. Clear sound, useful framing and a good performance are the priorities.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Use a steady camera or phone, a quiet room, a simple background and soft light on your face. Follow the casting brief on framing, orientation, file format, slate and deadline.

Work with a reader if the brief requires one. The reader should support the scene, not compete with it. Rehearse the relationship, intention and turning point before recording. Watch the take for clarity rather than endlessly judging your appearance.

Keep the recording private and send the requested file or secure link. You can ask about unreasonable requests, short notice or unsafe content. Equity's audition guidance is a useful starting point for knowing your boundaries.
