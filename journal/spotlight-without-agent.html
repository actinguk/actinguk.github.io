Do you need Spotlight or an agent to get auditions?

— No. But a professional casting profile and good representation can open doors that are harder to reach alone.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Spotlight currently has several eligibility routes: qualifying professional credits, qualifying performance training, or a recommendation from an eligible professional body member. Check its joining FAQ before paying for training or assuming you are ineligible.

If you are not eligible yet, self-submit to appropriate open calls and verified independent productions, and build credits that genuinely meet the platform's requirements. Background work does not automatically count as a featured acting credit.

An agent can put you forward for opportunities and negotiate terms, but representation is not a guarantee of work. A focused, self-represented actor can still develop a career. Review the cost and access terms of any platform before subscribing.
