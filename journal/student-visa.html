Can you work as an actor in the UK on a Student visa?

— Do not assume so. The Student route has specific restrictions that are especially important for drama students.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Current Home Office guidance says Students cannot be employed as entertainers and generally cannot be self-employed. Permission for ordinary part-time employment, where granted, is separate from those restrictions. Your course and individual conditions determine the hours of permitted employment.

A course performance, placement, volunteer activity, unpaid film or audition can involve different rules; calling something unpaid does not automatically make it permitted. Before accepting a professional engagement or undertaking activity outside your course, ask your university's international student adviser or a regulated immigration adviser.

Read your visa conditions and the current immigration rules. Do not rely on an agent, a friend's experience or a social media answer for permission to work.
