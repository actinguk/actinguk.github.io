What is the best survival job for an actor in London?

— The best job pays reliably and leaves enough energy and flexibility for acting.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Consider temp administration, hospitality, customer support, tutoring, events or other work you are qualified and legally permitted to do. Remote or hybrid work may help with self-tapes, but it does not automatically mean you can leave for auditions.

Ask about shift swaps, leave requests, notice, overtime and predictable hours before accepting. A regular part-time job plus a second income source can be more sustainable than constantly chasing casual shifts.

Calculate take-home pay, travel and the cost of time off. Avoid relying on a zero-income week being rescued by a booking. If your visa restricts freelance work or work as an entertainer, check your conditions before taking a side job.
