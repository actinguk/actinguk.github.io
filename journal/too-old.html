Are you too old to start acting at 30, 40 or 50?

— There is no universal expiry date on starting. Your training route and practical goals matter more than a deadline you invent for yourself.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

Identify the roles and genres that interest you now. Your previous work, language, lived experience and specialist skills may give you useful material to draw on. Do not pretend to be younger or claim experience you do not have.

Start with a course or regular class, record a scene and build a realistic CV. Research agencies and opportunities that include people at your career stage, and remember that casting needs people of many ages.

Set a goal for the next six months that you can control: improve technique, finish a scene, apply consistently or perform in a local production. Reassess your progress against that goal rather than comparing yourself with somebody who started as a child.
