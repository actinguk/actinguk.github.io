After Today revisits Bill Grundy at the Hope Theatre

Tim Connery’s play opens on 24th November, with Alex Dee as Grundy and Kevin McNally directing.

Acting UK editorial24th November 2026

The Hope Theatre in Islington opens After Today on 24th November, a two-character play by Tim Connery imagining the later career of broadcaster Bill Grundy. Stage D’Or presents the production, directed by Kevin McNally, to coincide with the fiftieth anniversary of Grundy’s television interview with the Sex Pistols.

The interview took place on 1st December 1976. Connery’s play moves the action forward several years, placing Grundy in a fictional television assignment about castles. It is a curious setting for a man whose most famous broadcasting encounter was rather less sedate.

Alex Dee plays Grundy and Tom Thornhill plays Julian, the director of the imagined programme. Luke Adamson is credited as production manager. The contrast between the television personality and the person responsible for getting a programme made gives the piece a dramatic relationship beyond its historical reference point.

The anniversary falls within the run itself, on 1st December. The premise invites us to consider an interview remembered largely for its guests from the perspective of the broadcaster who became inseparable from it. The invented later encounter should not be mistaken for a documentary reconstruction.

The Hope lists a preview on 24th November and performances until 5th December, at 7pm, with an age restriction of 18 and over. This is a preview, not a review.
