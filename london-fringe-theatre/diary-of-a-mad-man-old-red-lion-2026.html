Mark McAuley brings Gogol’s Diary of a Mad Man to the Old Red Lion

The Edinburgh Fringe transfer plays six performances in Islington from 24th to 28th November.

Acting UK editorial26th November 2026

Mark McAuley’s Diary of a Mad Man is playing at the Old Red Lion Theatre in Islington this week, following an Edinburgh Festival Fringe engagement. The actor directs and performs the hour-long solo production, which is scheduled for six London performances between 24th and 28th November.

The adaptation relocates Nikolai Gogol’s story to modern Ireland. Its protagonist works in a corporate environment where status is an obsession and professional disappointment increasingly determines his understanding of the world. Online attention becomes a particularly unreliable measure of his significance.

McAuley credits Derek Masterson as writer and describes the staging as incorporating multimedia and interaction with the audience. The adaptation draws a connection between Gogol’s account of an increasingly unstable consciousness and contemporary networks in which resentment can find reinforcement.

The performer’s official production page identifies this engagement as the London premiere and lists a matinee on 28th November. The absence of an interval makes this a concentrated encounter with one character. Our coverage is based on the published production information; we have not attended the performance.
