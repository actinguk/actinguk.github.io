Dick Whittington heads to Islington as King’s Head unveils its winter panto

Andrew Pollard’s family pantomime opens on 24th November, with Joseph Vella, Victoria Scone and River Medway in the cast.

Acting UK editorial23rd November 2026

London’s most famous fictional apprentice has a short journey to make: Dick Whittington and His Cat begins its winter run at the King’s Head Theatre in Islington on 24th November, in a new family pantomime written and directed by Andrew Pollard.

Joseph Vella plays Dick, with Patricia Wilkins as Alice Fitzgerald and Sé Carr as Ding-Dong and Elvis Pawsley. Ash Weir appears as Anne Semble. Victoria Scone returns as Dragpuss and River Medway plays Queen Rat, bringing a distinctly North London accent to a story in which the big city rarely behaves as advertised.

The plot makes comic use of the venue’s neighbourhood. Dick arrives in London expecting fame and fortune, only to find the streets far less golden than promised and the local rats rather more ambitious. The theatre describes a mix of musical numbers, familiar panto business and jokes pitched at a family audience.

Pollard returns for a third King’s Head pantomime, following Cinderella and Jack and the Beanstalk. The creative team listed by the venue includes set and costume designer Nicky Bunch, musical director George W. Parris, lighting designer Alex Lewer and sound designer Anna Short.

The current venue schedule lists performances from 24th November 2026 through 3rd January 2027, including a Pay What You Can preview on 25th November. This is a production preview based on the official schedule, not a review of a performance we have attended. The theatre now operates in a purpose-built space behind its historic Upper Street pub.
