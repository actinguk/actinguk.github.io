Elysium travels from Plymouth to the White Bear

A new two-act play exploring belonging and collective consciousness opens at the Kennington venue on 14th December.

Acting UK editorial28th November 2026

The White Bear Theatre has scheduled Elysium for a December engagement in Kennington. The new two-act play opens on 14th December and continues until 23rd December. London Pub Theatres describes a development process in Plymouth involving Theatre Royal Plymouth before the work’s journey to London.

The advertised themes include shared consciousness, freedom and the need to belong. They are expansive ideas for an intimate theatre, and the proximity of an audience may prove particularly interesting when the subject is the relationship between an individual and a group.

We are interested in the route the play has taken as much as its premise. New writing is rarely born fully formed in the venue where the public eventually encounters it. Development away from London and a subsequent small-scale production offer a valuable reminder of how work can move between regional and capital-based theatre communities.

The White Bear lists the running time as ninety minutes including an interval. Standard tickets are eighteen pounds, with concessions at sixteen pounds. Casting and detailed creative credits were not confirmed in the available venue listing, so we have not attributed them. This article is a preview of the forthcoming engagement.
