Fly, You Fools! brings a miniature Middle-earth to Southwark Playhouse

Recent Cutbacks’ Lord of the Rings parody opens at Southwark Playhouse Borough on 25th November.

Acting UK editorial22nd November 2026

Middle-earth is about to fit into a Southwark auditorium with rather fewer resources than Peter Jackson enjoyed. Fly, You Fools!, created by Recent Cutbacks, begins its London run at Southwark Playhouse Borough on 25th November 2026 and is scheduled until 16th January 2027, following two Edinburgh Festival Fringe seasons.

The company describes a live parody of the first Lord of the Rings film, assembled from three actors and a Foley artist. Mime, shadow puppetry and live sound effects are the working materials; the epic is compressed into a performance of roughly an hour. The premise depends on the audience recognising the film’s monumental imagery while enjoying the sheer economy of its replacement.

The venue credits Kristin McCarthy Parker as director, Blair Busbee for Foley design and Kelsey Didion for a cappella arrangements. Its production page says further cast and creative details are still to be announced. It would be premature to name a London company from photographs of previous stagings.

The announced captioned performance is on 16th December. The first performance on 25th November is advertised as a preview. We are not claiming to have seen the show; the interest lies in the actual London opening and a very particular kind of theatrical adaptation, where the machinery of illusion is deliberately left in full view.
