Fourteen Again brings Victoria Wood’s songs to the Menier

Ria Jones, Neil Morrissey and Sally Ann Triplett star in Tom MacRae’s musical, now previewing in London.

Acting UK editorial28th November 2026

Fourteen Again is in previews at the Menier Chocolate Factory following its first production in Bowness-on-Windermere. Jonathan O’Boyle directs the musical, which combines Victoria Wood’s songs with a new book by Tom MacRae. Opening night is scheduled for 2nd December.

Sally Ann Triplett plays Peggy, a woman whose encounter with an old friend unexpectedly returns her to adolescence. Ria Jones plays Lou and Neil Morrissey plays Duncan. The premise allows the show to approach friendship and regret through the rather uncomfortable business of revisiting one’s younger self.

Wood’s work often found comedy in the discrepancy between what people wanted to say and what they actually managed to utter. For a performer, that is fertile dramatic territory: the joke can arise from an entirely serious attempt to maintain dignity. We will be interested to see how the songs and MacRae’s story negotiate that balance.

The Menier engagement continues until 27th February 2027. This is a preview of the London production, rather than a review based on attendance.
