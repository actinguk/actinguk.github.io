Global Majority turns a job interview into a struggle for power

Chris Fung’s political thriller at the Bush Theatre features Jeremy Ang Jones and marks Cade Brannan’s professional stage debut.

Acting UK editorial28th November 2026

At the Bush Theatre, Chris Fung’s Global Majority begins with an interview whose stakes are considerably higher than securing a job. Brian Tran, played by Jeremy Ang Jones, is being tested for a position of extraordinary influence. Rupert Hands directs the thriller.

Edward Baker-Duly, Poppy Gilbert and Cade Brannan join Ang Jones in the company. The production marks Brannan’s professional stage debut after training at Mountview. The Bush lists Fung as the winner of the 2026 Ilfeld Prize for this play.

For actors, an interview is an appealing dramatic structure because every answer potentially changes the relationship in the room. The apparent subject of a conversation need not be its actual subject. A performer can be required to convey calculation, embarrassment and resistance without announcing any of them.

The production continues at the Bush’s Holloway Theatre until 16th January. The venue lists tickets from fifteen pounds and a running time of two hours and twenty minutes including an interval. This is an editorial preview, not a claim to have attended the production.
