Goblin arrives at Park Theatre with Derek Mitchell’s dark coming-of-age solo show

Derek Mitchell’s one-person production plays at Park Theatre until 5th December.

Acting UK editorial23rd November 2026

Derek Mitchell’s Goblin is listed at Park Theatre from 23rd November to 5th December, bringing a darkly comic solo performance about adolescence, secrecy and exploitation to Finsbury Park. Written and performed by Mitchell, the production is directed by Ellie BW and Sammy Glover.

The story begins in Decatur, Illinois, in 2007. Fifteen-year-old Eliot is negotiating a difficult home life and the threat of being outed at school. His imagined goblins offer companionship and a private audience for the thoughts he cannot safely share. An encounter with an older reality television personality named Max draws him into a destructive relationship.

That premise makes the show more complicated than its mischievous title might suggest. The venue describes a performance built around shifting characters, voices and physical transformations. A solo actor must make those changes legible without asking the audience to forget the person at the centre of the story.

Park Theatre lists a running time of seventy minutes without an interval and an age recommendation of sixteen and over. Its content guidance includes grooming and exploitation of a minor, sexual material and strong language; prospective audiences should read the full venue warnings before booking.

Earlier preview and press performances were listed for 17th and 18th November. This is a production preview based on the theatre’s published information, not an account of having seen the show. The theatre’s booking page remains the authoritative source for performance times and access details.
