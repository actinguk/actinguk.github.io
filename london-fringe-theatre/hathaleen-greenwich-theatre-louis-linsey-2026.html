Hathaleen concludes its Greenwich Theatre studio run

Louis Linsey’s play, developed from his experiences in the West Bank, reaches its final scheduled performance on 26th November.

Acting UK editorial26th November 2026

Hathaleen: The Unexpected Journey to Palestine reaches the last scheduled night of its Greenwich Theatre studio engagement on 26th November. Louis Linsey wrote and directs the satirical drama, which the venue describes as drawing on his time teaching and undertaking protective-presence work in the West Bank.

The fictional narrative follows Leon, played by Benjamin Robinson, who travels to Palestine in an attempt to repair a failed relationship. The encounter becomes the basis for a story about political convictions, personal motives and the distance between an outsider’s expectations and the lives of the people he meets.

The company includes Ani Russo as Thea, Salsabil Al-Masri as Shams, Jad Dirani as Awdah, Basil Badi as Ahmed, Laurenz Prader as Yinon, James Ross Turner as the Soldier and Arman Khan as Muhammad.

According to the venue, the production pays tribute to Palestinian teacher and activist Awdah Hathaleen and follows earlier runs at The Source and Arches Lane Theatre. The Greenwich engagement runs for 95 minutes. This report concerns the production and its stated intentions; it makes no claim of attendance or independent judgement of its performance.
