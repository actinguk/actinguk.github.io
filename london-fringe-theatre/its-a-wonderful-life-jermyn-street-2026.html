It’s a Wonderful Life goes on air at Jermyn Street Theatre

Jessica Lazar’s radio-on-stage production runs from 19th November to 3rd January, with six multi-roling performers.

Acting UK editorial25th November 2026

Jermyn Street Theatre has turned its basement stage into a period radio studio for It’s a Wonderful Life, running until 3rd January. Jessica Lazar directs Anthony E. Palermo’s adaptation of Frank Capra’s film, itself based on Philip Van Doren Stern’s The Greatest Gift.

Six performers are responsible for creating the world of George Bailey through voices, music and sound effects. Griffin Stevens plays George, Iniki Mariano plays Mary, and Stefan Bednarczyk plays Clarence as well as providing piano accompaniment. Mary Higgins, David Webber and Lachlan Werner take several other roles.

The conceit makes the mechanics of storytelling part of the performance: the audience sees actors constructing the broadcast while following the fictional town and its inhabitants. For a small theatre, the radio studio offers a way of suggesting a much larger world without pretending to reproduce the original film’s scenery.

Tina Torbey is the set designer, Anna Lewis the costume designer and Cheng Keng the lighting designer; Odinn Orn Hilmarsson composes and designs sound. The theatre has scheduled relaxed performances on 1st and 9th December and a captioned performance on 16th December. This is a preview, not an assessment of the staging.
