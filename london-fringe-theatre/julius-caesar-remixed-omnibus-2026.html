Intermission Youth puts Julius Caesar in a modern classroom at Omnibus

Darren Raymond’s play, directed by Stephanie Badaru, continues at the Clapham theatre until 5th December.

Acting UK editorial25th November 2026

Intermission Youth’s Julius Caesar Remixed continues its run at Omnibus Theatre in Clapham until 5th December. Written by Darren Raymond and directed by Stephanie Badaru, the play places a school production of Shakespeare inside a contemporary story of rival London schools.

The arrival of a new pupil, Malik, unsettles a class already dealing with a recent death. When he is cast as Caesar, rehearsals begin to reflect tensions in the classroom itself. The play-within-a-play structure is a way to explore how suspicion and loyalty can travel between the imagined Rome and the students’ lives.

The company includes Jamal Abdul and Nathan Morgan as Malik/Caesar, Omari Green and Victor A. Daniels as Elijah/Brutus, and Gabrielle Assuman and Rebecca Maddy as Zoe/Cassius. The published credits also list Federay Holmes as associate director, Ruth Badila as set and costume designer, and Rajiv Pattani as lighting designer.

Omnibus advertises a ninety-minute performance followed by a twenty-minute discussion. Its listing gives an age recommendation of ten and over and a number of content advisories. The production offers young performers a substantial ensemble setting, with backstage trainees also credited. This is a production preview based on the company and venue’s information.
