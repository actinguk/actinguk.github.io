Majenin brings a Palestinian romantic comedy to Theatre503

Marena Riyad’s Edinburgh Fringe two-hander runs at the Battersea venue from 23rd to 28th November.

Acting UK editorial24th November 2026

Kufiya Comedy’s Majenin – A Palestinian Love Story began its London engagement at Theatre503 on 23rd November. Written by Marena Riyad, the two-hander follows an Edinburgh Fringe run and plays at the Battersea venue until 28th November.

Its story follows a relationship between two Palestinian artists across New York, London and Palestine. The characters negotiate cultural obligations, distance and uncertainty while trying to do something sufficiently complicated in its own right: fall in love.

Riyad, whom the venue credits as writer and performer, works across comedy and theatre. The production blends stand-up with theatrical storytelling, allowing a romantic narrative to make room for a question about what kinds of stories Palestinian characters are permitted to inhabit.

Rather than treating its characters solely as representatives of political circumstances, the play puts their emotional lives at the centre. That is the ambition described in the venue’s material, not an assessment of the performance.

Theatre503 lists evening performances at 7.30pm, a running time of 75 minutes without an interval and age guidance of 16 and over. The engagement forms part of its winter short-run programme.
