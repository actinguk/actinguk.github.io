Mary Bennet takes the lead in Park Theatre’s Christmas at Pemberley

Sarah Meadows directs the UK premiere of Lauren Gunderson and Margot Melcon’s Austen sequel from 2nd December.

Acting UK editorial26th November 2026

Park Theatre in Finsbury Park is preparing the UK premiere of Miss Bennet: Christmas at Pemberley, beginning on 2nd December. Sarah Meadows directs Lauren Gunderson and Margot Melcon’s play, presented by the theatre with Adam Blanshay Productions and Eric Kuhn Productions.

The play imagines a festive family gathering after Pride and Prejudice, moving the focus from Elizabeth to her sister Mary. The arrival of Arthur de Bourgh gives Mary a chance to escape the narrow expectations her family has placed upon her.

Chloe Wade plays Mary opposite Keaton Guimarães-Tolley as Arthur. The company includes Cash Holland as Jane, Nadi Kemp-Sayfi as Elizabeth, Kara Lynch as Lydia, Yasmine Meaden as Anne, Jack Michael Stacey as Darcy and George Rowlands as Bingley.

Amy Jane Cook designs the set and costumes, with Jodie Underwood responsible for lighting and Matt Huxley for composition and sound. The run is scheduled to end on 10th January 2027. Park Theatre also lists captioned, audio-described and dementia-friendly performances. This is a production preview, not an assessment of the staging.
