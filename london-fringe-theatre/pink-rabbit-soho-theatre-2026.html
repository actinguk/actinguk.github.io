Pink Rabbit transfers to Soho Theatre after its Edinburgh debut

Farah Ashraf’s solo play, directed by Alessandro Babalola, runs in Soho Upstairs from 24th November to 12th December.

Acting UK editorial24th November 2026

Farah Ashraf brings Pink Rabbit to Soho Theatre on 24th November following its Edinburgh Festival Fringe debut. The solo production runs in Soho Upstairs until 12th December, directed by Alessandro Babalola, who is also credited as dramaturg.

Ashraf’s protagonist Amna maintains two apparently incompatible versions of herself: the dutiful daughter at home and an online persona whose appeal depends on being watched. Her efforts to keep the two lives separate provide the dramatic mechanism of a story about desire, religious expectations and digital attention.

A solo show is an apt form for a character negotiating how she is perceived. The premise leaves the performer with the task of making both versions of Amna recognisable as parts of the same person, rather than two convenient caricatures.

Soho credits Aoife Smyth as associate director, Daljinder Johal as producer, Lolly Whitney Low as set designer and Naomi Shanson for lighting and technical stage management. The venue lists the production as a recipient of the 2026 Bestie! Award for Best Solo Show and the Shedload-of-Future Fund.

The run is advertised at 70 minutes, with an age recommendation of 16 and over. This preview is based on the theatre’s production information, not a first-hand review.
