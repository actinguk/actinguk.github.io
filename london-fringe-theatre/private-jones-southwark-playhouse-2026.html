Private Jones brings signed language and sound together on stage

Rose Ayling-Ellis and James Spence feature in Marshall Pailet’s musical opening at Southwark Playhouse Elephant on 14th December.

Acting UK editorial27th November 2026

Private Jones opens at Southwark Playhouse Elephant on 14th December, with performances scheduled until 30th January 2027. Written and directed by Marshall Pailet, the musical draws inspiration from the life of Gomer Jones.

James Spence plays Gomer, alongside Rose Ayling-Ellis as the Storyteller, Bronwyn James as King and Jacob Young as Trystan. The story follows a deaf man who conceals his deafness during military recruitment and discovers an unexpected community among his fellow soldiers.

The production combines English, Welsh and British Sign Language, with deaf, hard-of-hearing and hearing performers. Captions form part of the scenic design throughout the run, while Foley effects contribute to the musical soundscape. A BSL-interpreted performance is scheduled for 5th January.

For actors, the approach offers the possibility of treating communication as part of dramatic composition rather than an extra layer fitted to a finished performance. The venue advises that certain restricted-view seats can affect sightlines to the captions. Check its access information before booking.
