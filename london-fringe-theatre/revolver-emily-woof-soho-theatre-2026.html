Emily Woof’s REVOLVER moves between Beatlemania and the present

Hamish McColl directs Woof’s Edinburgh transfer at Soho Theatre until 5th December.

Acting UK editorial27th November 2026

Emily Woof’s REVOLVER has arrived at Soho Theatre following its Edinburgh Festival run. Directed by Hamish McColl, the seventy-minute piece plays on Dean Street from 24th November to 5th December.

The title points back to the Beatles’ 1966 album, but the production takes a wider view. Three strands connect a present-day television researcher, a young fan writing to John Lennon in 1966 and Valerie Solanas seeking an opportunity for her work in Andy Warhol’s New York.

There is an interesting theatrical tension between a culture that remembers the sixties as a moment of emancipation and the experiences of women navigating its hierarchies. Moving between periods can unsettle the assumption that progress occurs in a straight line. The distance between a fan and an icon is not necessarily the same as the distance between a woman and power.

Soho Theatre recommends the production for audiences aged fourteen and above. Tickets and content advice are available through the official listing. This is a production preview, rather than an assessment of the performance.
