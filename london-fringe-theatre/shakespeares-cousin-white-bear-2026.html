Shakespeare’s Cousin enters its final week at the White Bear

Jonathan Moore’s new play about Robert Southwell runs in Kennington until 28th November.

Acting UK editorial25th November 2026

AMDG Productions’ Shakespeare’s Cousin is in its final week at the White Bear Theatre, with the Kennington run ending on 28th November. Written and directed by Jonathan Moore, the play received its world premiere at the venue this month.

At its centre is Robert Southwell, the poet and Jesuit priest whose life brings the religious conflicts of Elizabethan England into the foreground. Rather than making Shakespeare the sole focus of the evening, Moore places another writer and the dangers of his convictions at the heart of the drama.

The cast is Danny Solomon as Southwell, Robin Kingsland as Richard Topcliffe and Alexander Townson as Sir Oliver and Shakespeare, according to the production credits reproduced by TheatreReviews.Design. Max Bancroft is assistant director and associate producer; Gabriel Burns designs lighting and sound, and Violet Gausden designs the set and supervises costumes.

The White Bear lists a two-hour running time, including an interval, and restricts admission to adults. This is a production feature based on the venue listing and published credits, not a first-hand review.
