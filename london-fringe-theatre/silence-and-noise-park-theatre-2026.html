The Silence and the Noise returns to Park Theatre with a Papatango Prize pedigree

Tom Powell’s two-hander, directed by George Turvey, runs from 25th November to 12th December.

Acting UK editorial23rd November 2026

Tom Powell’s The Silence and the Noise comes to Park Theatre from 25th November to 12th December, directed by George Turvey. The play won the 2021 Papatango Prize, selected from more than 1,400 submitted scripts, and is presented by Papatango Theatre Company and Pentabus Theatre in association with the venue.

At the centre are Daize and Ben, two teenagers whose circumstances have left them negotiating adult dangers long before they have much control over their own lives. Daize is trying to protect her mother from Ben’s drug-dealing boss. Their developing connection offers a fragile counterweight to the forces around them.

The title suggests competing registers: the pressure of a threatening environment and the smaller, quieter negotiations between young people who do not have the luxury of speaking freely. That is an interpretation of the premise, not a description of a performance we have attended.

The theatre lists a seventy-minute running time without an interval. It is an economical format for a story whose stakes depend on the changing relationship between two characters rather than the machinery of a large set.

The production’s return also puts an award-winning new-writing script back in front of London audiences. Awards can introduce a play, but they cannot perform it; the present production will have to make its own case in the room. Full scheduling and booking information is available from Park Theatre.
