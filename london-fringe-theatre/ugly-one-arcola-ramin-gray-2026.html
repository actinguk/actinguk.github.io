Ramin Gray revisits The Ugly One at Arcola Theatre

Marius von Mayenburg’s satire of appearance and identity plays in Arcola’s Studio 2 until 19th December.

Acting UK editorial23rd November 2026

Marius von Mayenburg’s The Ugly One is running in Studio 2 at Arcola Theatre until 19th December, in a production directed by Ramin Gray and presented by Broken Nose Theatre. The run began on 17th November. Nearly two decades after the play’s Royal Court premiere, its central joke has lost none of its uncomfortable potential.

Lette learns that the people closest to him regard him as ugly. Surgery transforms his appearance, and with it his prospects at work and in his relationships. Then the new face ceases to be exclusive. In a world where desirability is treated like an industrial process, the supposedly perfect product is soon available to somebody else.

The Arcola describes four actors moving between eight characters, sometimes in the middle of a sentence. That doubling is not merely an economical fringe-theatre solution: it belongs to the satire’s concern with interchangeability, status and the precarious value of being considered unique.

Gray returns to a text whose surface is brisk and comic but whose subject is severe. We are surrounded by technologies that promise to correct or optimise appearances, yet the play’s most pointed question concerns the people who get to define the defect in the first place.

The venue lists performances through 19th December and identifies the production as sold out at the time of its listing. Availability can change, so check directly with Arcola. This is an event-led preview informed by the official production page, not a first-hand review.
