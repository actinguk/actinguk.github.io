Barbara Broccoli takes the helm at the NFTS

The Bond producer becomes the National Film and Television School’s first woman president, as the school prepares to open a new building in January.

Acting UK editorial 14 November 2026

The National Film and Television School has appointed Barbara Broccoli as its president, the first woman to hold the position. The announcement on 12 November also confirms that David Puttnam, whom she succeeds, will remain associated with the school as president emeritus.

Broccoli’s career is closely tied to the James Bond films, but this appointment concerns the next generation of British screen talent rather than the next Bond. One of her first engagements in the role will be the formal opening of the Cubby Broccoli Building at Beaconsfield in January 2027. The development adds teaching and creative facilities and accommodation for students.

For actors, the NFTS is worth following even if film school is not on the agenda. Film students and emerging directors need performers to develop their work, and relationships established on a well-run short film can continue into professional projects. The useful thing to watch is the work coming out of the school, not simply the prominent name attached to its presidency.

The appointment is not an open casting call or an announcement of new acting courses. Anyone interested in working with student filmmakers should follow the school’s official channels, check the individual production brief and establish the terms of any engagement before agreeing to take part.
