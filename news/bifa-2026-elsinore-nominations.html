Elsinore leads the 2026 British Independent Film Awards nominations

Simon Stone’s Ian Charleson drama leads the BIFA field with 12 nominations ahead of December’s ceremony.

Acting UK editorial22nd November 2026

The 2026 British Independent Film Awards have a particularly theatrical front-runner. Elsinore, Simon Stone’s film about actor Ian Charleson’s final Hamlet while living with AIDS, leads the nominations with twelve, according to the BFI’s announcement of 3rd November. The awards will be presented at the Roundhouse on 6th December, with craft winners announced on 24th November.

Among Elsinore’s nominations are Best British Independent Film, Best Director and Best Screenplay, alongside acting recognition for Andrew Scott and Billie Piper. It is a conspicuous reminder that a story about an actor’s work can itself become a major showcase for screen performance. The film’s subject is not merely Shakespeare’s most famous part but the personal and professional circumstances in which it was performed.

The BFI says films it backed received thirty-nine nominations overall. Clio Barnard’s I See Buildings Fall Like Lightning has eight, while Ashley Walters’ directorial debut Animol has seven. Both feature in a field that also includes Queen at Sea, Effi o Blaenau and Mike Leigh’s Tender Loving Care in the Best British Independent Film category.

There are two distinct stories here: the prominence of a drama about theatre history, and the breadth of contemporary British independent filmmaking competing beside it. The nominations are not results; the ceremony is still to come. For now, the shortlist offers a useful snapshot of the performers and filmmakers whose work has caught the industry’s attention this year.
