Bradford venue faces closure

Theatre in the Mill is due to close on 31 March 2027 after the University of Bradford withdrew funding.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What this means for actors

This is a reminder that the health of regional venues affects rehearsal, performance and development opportunities. Follow the original report and the venue for confirmed changes; do not infer that every planned event is cancelled.
