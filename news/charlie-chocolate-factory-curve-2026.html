Curve’s Charlie and the Chocolate Factory puts ensemble craft in the spotlight

Leicester’s Christmas musical releases its first production photographs, with Jonny Fines leading a company that includes young performers and swings.

Acting UK editorial28th November 2026

The first production photographs of Curve’s Charlie and the Chocolate Factory appeared on 27th November, just under a week into its Leicester run. Nikolai Foster directs the musical, with Jonny Fines as Willy Wonka and four young actors sharing the role of Charlie Bucket.

A production of this scale depends on rather more than the central performance. Jordan Dixon makes his professional debut as an onstage swing alongside Rachel Jayne Picar, with Joseph Craig and Tori McDougall covering from offstage. Olivia Laydon handles casting for Jill Green Casting.

For actors, the prominence of the swing team is a useful reminder that versatility is not merely a line on a CV. In a musical built around illusion, choreography and puppetry, covering a part requires familiarity with the machinery of the evening as well as its emotional shape.

The production runs until 16th January 2027. Alongside the performances, Curve is offering educational activity for schools, including a Theatre Day on 10th December. The photographs are a first glimpse of a substantial regional production, not a substitute for seeing the work itself.
