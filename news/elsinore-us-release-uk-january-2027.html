Elsinore begins its US cinema release ahead of January’s UK opening

Simon Stone’s film about Ian Charleson reached selected US cinemas on 20th November; its UK release is set for 29th January.

Acting UK editorial22nd November 2026

Elsinore has reached another point in its release calendar. The BFI’s official announcement lists a selected United States cinema release through Focus Features on 20th November 2026, with STUDIOCANAL due to release the film across the UK on 29th January 2027. The picture had its European premiere opening the seventieth BFI London Film Festival on 7th October.

Directed by Simon Stone from a screenplay by Stephen Beresford, Elsinore dramatises actor Ian Charleson’s preparations for Hamlet at the National Theatre as he faced serious illness. It brings an unusually specific piece of British theatre history into the cinema, with the act of preparing a performance at the centre of the narrative.

The film’s staggered release matters to UK audiences following the festival: a gala screening is not the same thing as a nationwide cinema opening. The US date is a limited release, and UK viewers should not mistake it for the start of general local screenings. The BFI identifies the January date as the planned UK release.

With awards nominations now also attracting attention, the film sits at the intersection of theatre memory, cinema distribution and contemporary performance. We will reserve critical judgement for the work itself. The confirmed development today is its release schedule, not a claim about box-office results or audience response.
