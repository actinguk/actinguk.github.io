Equity and ITC agree three years of minimum pay increases

Independent theatre’s new agreement sets minimum weekly rates from April 2027, alongside plans for ethical casting and AI policies.

Acting UK editorial27th November 2026

Independent theatre has a new set of figures to work with. On 23rd November, Equity announced that members had endorsed a three-year agreement with the Independent Theatre Council (ITC). The first increase takes effect on 1st April 2027.

The minimum weekly rate for performers and stage managers will be £650 in 2027–28, £670 in 2028–29 and £690 in 2029–30. Wages, freelance rates and allowances are due to rise by three per cent each year, with weekly rates rounded upwards. Equity reported ninety per cent support among those voting.

An early settlement has a practical virtue: companies can budget for employment costs in funding applications instead of guessing at them. For performers, the agreed minimum is a starting point for negotiation, not a ceiling. Check whether an individual engagement is covered before relying on these figures.

The parties also plan further work on ethical casting, AI, creative rights and practices around costume, hair and make-up, as well as agreements for intimacy and movement directors. These are plans for further negotiation, not completed new protections.
