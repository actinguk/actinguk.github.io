Yana Perrault completes The Greatest Showman’s principal cast

Disney’s stage adaptation adds Perrault as Anne Wheeler ahead of its Theatre Royal Drury Lane opening in 2027.

Acting UK editorial28th November 2026

Disney Theatrical Group completed the principal casting of The Greatest Showman on 26th November, announcing Yana Perrault as Anne Wheeler. The stage adaptation begins its London engagement at Theatre Royal Drury Lane on 14th April 2027.

Perrault joins a company including Samantha Barks as Charity Barnum, Ben Joyce as Phillip Carlyle, Vajèn van den Bosch as Jenny Lind and Malinda Parris as Lettie Lutz. Casey Nicholaw directs and choreographs, with Tim Federle writing the book and Benj Pasek and Justin Paul providing the songs.

The particularly interesting challenge for the company is the audience’s familiarity with the film. A recognisable score may draw people into the theatre, but it also makes the individual performer’s interpretation unusually exposed. The musical’s characters will need to have an existence beyond the screen performances that introduced them.

The scale of the creative team, which includes specialists in circus and illusion, suggests an evening in which acting will have to coexist with elaborate physical and technical demands. Opening night is set for 5th May, with the production initially booking until 2nd October.
