Joel Montague turns green as Leeds Playhouse stages a new Grinch

Tom Wright directs the Broadway musical’s new UK production in the Quarry, with performances continuing until 16th January.

Acting UK editorial24th November 2026

Leeds Playhouse has begun performances of its new UK staging of Dr Seuss’s How The Grinch Stole Christmas! The Musical, with Joel Montague in the title role. The run began on 19th November in the Quarry and continues until 16th January 2027.

Artistic director Tom Wright directs the show, produced with Running Subway. Timothy Mason wrote the book and lyrics, and Mel Marvin composed the music. The Grinch’s dog Max narrates the story, as festive preparations in Who-ville provide the object of his master’s considerable irritation.

The cast includes Jack Arnold as Young Max, Jessica Barton as Betty Lou Who, Emily Barnett-Salter as Auntie Who, Alison Fitzjohn as Grandma Who and Luke Latchman as Papa Who. A young company joins the professional cast.

Simon Higlett designs the set and costumes, Tinovimbanashe Sibanda choreographs and Livi Van Warmelo is musical director. Montague’s previous festive outing was Elf in the West End, a rather different proposition from playing a character determined to stop the celebrations.

The press night is scheduled for 26th November, according to WhatsOnStage. With a 90-minute running time and age guidance of four and over, the production is aimed at family audiences. This is a report on the opening run, not a performance review.
