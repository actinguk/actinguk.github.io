Hamnet leaves Stratford for its first UK tour

The RSC and Neal Street stage adaptation will begin a nationwide tour in Salford in September 2027, with casting still to come.

Acting UK editorial 16th November 2026

Shakespeare’s family is about to spend rather more time away from Stratford. The Royal Shakespeare Company and Neal Street announced on 12th November that their stage adaptation of Maggie O’Farrell’s Hamnet will undertake its first UK tour, beginning at the Lowry in Salford on 25th September 2027.

Adapted by Lolita Chakrabarti and directed by Erica Whyman, the play centres on Agnes Hathaway and William Shakespeare as they contend with the death of their son. It premiered at the Swan Theatre in 2023 before transferring to the West End and touring the United States in 2026.

The announced itinerary includes Newcastle, Truro, Norwich, Nottingham, Canterbury, Birmingham, Glasgow and Bath before Christmas. The tour resumes in 2028, with stops including Belfast, York, Wimbledon and Southampton. Further dates are expected.

For actors, the production is a useful example of the life a successful adaptation can have beyond its original engagement. Its theatrical demands are distinct from those of the film: the intimacy of a family’s grief must remain legible in a succession of different auditoriums. The RSC has not announced the touring cast, so performers should treat this as a development to follow rather than an invitation to apply.
