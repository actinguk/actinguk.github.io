The History Boys launches an open search for eight West End pupils

Nicholas Hytner’s return to Alan Bennett’s play is inviting unrepresented actors to audition in London and Leeds.

Acting UK editorial23rd November 2026

The forthcoming West End return of Alan Bennett’s The History Boys is taking an unusually direct route to its schoolroom. Producers Brian and Dayna Lee, alongside director Nicholas Hytner, have launched an open-submissions search for the eight pupil roles, according to an announcement reported by WhatsOnStage on 18th November.

The invitation is directed at male-presenting performers aged eighteen or over who are not professionally represented. Applicants may be of any ethnicity, but must be able to portray an eighteen-year-old schoolboy convincingly. The roles named in the search are Akthar, Crowther, Dakin, Lockwood, Posner, Rudge, Scripps and Timms.

More than 150 actors are expected to be shortlisted for auditions. The announced audition dates are 27th November in London and 4th December in Leeds. Those are audition dates, not the production’s opening nights; applicants should consult the original announcement for submission requirements and any subsequent updates.

The casting decision is notable because The History Boys has a formidable history of introducing performers to wider audiences. Its original stage and screen company helped establish a generation of familiar names. A new cast will inevitably invite comparisons, but a casting search is not a promise of instant stardom, nor should the play be treated as a museum of its first performers.

For unrepresented actors, the substantive news is the availability of a route into a high-profile production that does not begin with an agent’s inbox. We will follow the eventual casting announcement rather than invent names before they are chosen.
