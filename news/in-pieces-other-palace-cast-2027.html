In Pieces assembles its cast for a London stage production

Joey Contreras’s musical follows six interlocking stories of love at The Other Palace from 6th February 2027.

Acting UK editorial27th November 2026

The cast of Joey Contreras’s In Pieces was announced this week, ahead of a six-week engagement at The Other Palace. Previews begin on 6th February, opening is scheduled for 16th February and the run ends on 21st March 2027.

Ailsa Davidson plays Sam, Elliott Evans is Grey, Collette Guitart is Alex, Natalie Kassanga is River, Max Mirza plays Peter and Jordan Walker is Charlie. Samuel Levene and Sophia Duncan join the ensemble, with Sameer Dhanjee and Rebecca Luter as swings. Martha Geelan directs.

Contreras wrote the book, music and lyrics. Rather than fastening the story to one romance, the musical follows six people through several periods of their lives. For actors, this suggests the interesting problem of making a character’s earlier choices recognisable without treating adulthood as simply an older version of adolescence.

The songs already have a life outside the theatre. A full stage production offers a different proposition: a continuous dramatic evening in which the performers can build relationships across musical numbers. February will reveal how the material works in that form.
