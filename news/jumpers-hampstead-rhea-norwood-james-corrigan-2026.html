Stoppard’s Jumpers begins its Hampstead revival with Rhea Norwood and James Corrigan

Blanche McIntyre directs the comedy’s first London staging in more than two decades, with previews starting on 25th November.

Acting UK editorial25th November 2026

Tom Stoppard’s Jumpers begins previews at Hampstead Theatre on 25th November. Blanche McIntyre directs the 1972 comedy, which the theatre says has not received a London staging in more than twenty years. The press night is scheduled for 7th December.

Rhea Norwood, known to television audiences from Heartstopper, plays Dorothy opposite James Corrigan as George. Jude Akuwudike, Tim McMullan, Scarlett Stitt and Jason Thorpe join them, alongside eight performers credited as jumpers.

Stoppard puts a moral philosopher in the middle of a peculiarly theatrical domestic catastrophe: a party involving music and acrobatics, a murder and the arrival of the police. George’s attempt to finish a philosophical paper takes place against an increasingly uncooperative reality.

Robert Innes Hopkins designs the production, with Guy Hoare responsible for lighting, George Dennis for sound and Tim Sutton for music. Tim Claydon directs movement and circus work; Anna Cooper is the casting director. The main-stage run continues until 23rd January 2027. We have not attended the revival.
