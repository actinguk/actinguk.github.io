Julianne Pundan to make her West End debut in Miss Saigon

The principal cast announced on 26th November reunites touring performers for the musical’s return to the Prince Edward Theatre.

Acting UK editorial27th November 2026

More than a decade after its previous London engagement, Miss Saigon returns to the Prince Edward Theatre. Producer Cameron Mackintosh announced the initial principal cast on 26th November. The run is scheduled from 13th May 2027 to 8th January 2028.

Seann Miley Moore returns as the Engineer, Julianne Pundan as Kim, Jack Kane as Chris and Emily Langham as Ellen. Dex Lee joins as John. A second performer sharing Kim and other principal casting will be announced later.

For Pundan, the engagement marks a West End debut following her professional debut on the UK and Ireland tour. The producer notes her 2026 Stage Debut Awards and UK Theatre Awards successes. Touring can be a substantial artistic destination in itself, rather than merely preparation for London.

Returning to a role in a different theatre changes the circumstances of performance: the room, audience and colleagues can all shift an actor’s relationship to the material. This is a confirmed casting announcement, not an open audition.
