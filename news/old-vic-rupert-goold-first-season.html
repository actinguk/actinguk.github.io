The Old Vic opens a new chapter with Rupert Goold’s first season

Four productions lead the incoming artistic director’s 2027 programme, with public booking due to open on 16 November.

Acting UK editorial 14 November 2026

The Old Vic’s first programme under Rupert Goold offers a useful glimpse of the kind of acting work its new leadership intends to put on the main stage. The theatre announced the first four productions on 10 November; general booking opens on Monday 16 November.

Goold directs Damian Lewis in Henry V, running from 26 January to 27 March 2027. In April, Rebecca Frecknall brings her contemporary reworking of Strindberg’s Miss Julie to the theatre, with Milly Alcock taking the lead. The summer brings Stephen Sondheim and John Weidman’s Assassins, also directed by Goold, followed by Arinzé Kene in the solo piece Kohlhaas.

It is a strikingly varied programme for performers to study: verse, contemporary adaptation, musical theatre and a one-person show all demand different kinds of technical control. The choice of work also points to the value of being able to move between classical text and more experimental approaches to storytelling.

The announcements are not invitations to audition. Additional casting information should be followed through the theatre and the casting professionals associated with individual productions. For now, the immediate date for the diary is the public ticket release on 16 November.
