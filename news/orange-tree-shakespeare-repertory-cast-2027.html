Orange Tree brings together a Shakespeare repertory company

Roger Allam and Freddie Fox lead a shared ensemble in King Lear and Richard II, directed by Tom Littler next spring.

Acting UK editorial28th November 2026

The Orange Tree Theatre confirmed the full company for its Shakespeare double bill on 27th November. Tom Littler will direct King Lear and Richard II in repertory, with Roger Allam and Freddie Fox appearing in both productions.

Allam plays Lear and the Duke of York; Fox takes Edmund and Richard. Arthur Hughes and Amber James are joined by performers including Aaron Anthony, Dorothea Myer-Bennett, Robert Mountford and Dominic Rowan. Casting is by Matilda James.

Repertory offers an unusually revealing test of an ensemble. Watching the same actor occupy contrasting positions in two political worlds can alter our understanding of both. The performer has to establish a new character without relying on the audience having forgotten the previous one.

Lear starts previews on 15th February and Richard II on 18th February 2027. Both open on 3rd March and continue until 10th April, before transferring to Theatre Royal Bath. These are Littler’s final productions as the Richmond theatre’s artistic director.
