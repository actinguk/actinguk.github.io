Phantom’s fortieth year brings a new trio to His Majesty’s Theatre

Kimberly Blake, Kate Graham and Luke Kempner join the West End production from 23rd November, with Ashley Gilmour returning as Raoul.

Acting UK editorial23rd November 2026

The Phantom of the Opera has changed the company without changing its address. From 23rd November, three new principal performers join the musical at His Majesty’s Theatre, a few weeks after the long-running production marked its fortieth anniversary.

Kimberly Blake takes over the role of Carlotta, Kate Graham becomes Madame Giry and Luke Kempner joins as Monsieur André. Ashley Gilmour also returns as Raoul. The announced company includes Jordan Donica as the Phantom and Beatrice Penny-Touré as Christine Daaé.

The switch is more than the usual reshuffle of names on a cast board. A musical running for four decades has the unusual task of staying recognisable while making room for performers to find their own version of a part. The familiar chandelier is one thing; the people beneath it cannot stand still forever.

The new appointments were announced on 5th November, with the changes taking effect on 23rd November. They are cast changes at an existing West End production, not a new opening or a newly announced tour. Casting can change during a long run, so visitors should consult the venue before booking for a particular performer.

Following the anniversary celebrations, the production continues at His Majesty’s Theatre on Haymarket. The fresh company offers audiences another way into a musical whose longevity might otherwise encourage everyone to assume they already know what is on stage.
