The Watermill brings Pinocchio back with Jax Guerrero in the title role

The Newbury theatre’s festive revival begins on 25th November, with returning performers and a new lead.

Acting UK editorial23rd November 2026

The Watermill Theatre in Newbury is preparing to revive its staging of Pinocchio from 25th November to 3rd January 2027. The production, first seen at the venue in 2024, adapts Michael Morpurgo’s retelling of the familiar wooden puppet’s adventures, with a new lead performer and several returning company members.

Jax Guerrero takes the title role, while Christopher Bianchi returns as Geppetto, Fred Double as Cricket, Eddy Payne as Cat and Jacoba Williams as Carabiniere. Polly Lister plays Signora Geppetto and Fox, Megan Keaveny appears as the Good Fairy and an onstage swing, and James Hall is the onstage musical director.

Simon Reade’s adaptation has music and lyrics by Chris Larner. Indiana Lown-Collins and Elle While co-direct; Marc Parrett’s puppetry and the production’s use of household objects are part of its distinctive theatrical vocabulary. The press performance is scheduled for 28th November, three days after the run begins.

The return is a reminder that regional theatres are not merely stopping points for work made elsewhere. The Watermill has built a recognisable form of intimate, inventive musical storytelling in which actors and musicians share responsibility for creating the world in front of the audience.

The announced dates and cast come from the production’s casting coverage. They do not establish how the revival has been received: any critical judgement about this new run must wait until performances have taken place.
