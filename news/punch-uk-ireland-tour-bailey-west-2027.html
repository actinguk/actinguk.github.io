Bailey West makes his stage debut in the touring cast of Punch

Nottingham Playhouse announced the cast on 19th November for James Graham’s play, touring from January to April 2027.

Acting UK editorial25th November 2026

Nottingham Playhouse has announced the company for the 2027 tour of James Graham’s Punch. Nottingham actor Bailey West will make his stage debut as Jacob, in Adam Penford’s production, which begins at the Playhouse on 25th January.

Graham’s play draws on Jacob Dunne’s memoir Right from Wrong, following the consequences of a fatal assault and the subsequent relationship between Dunne and the bereaved parents. The touring production brings the story back to the city in which it originated.

Joining West are Gail Kemp, Sally Ann Matthews, Mark Meadows and Mica Ricketts. Elan Butler returns as Raf and Sam; Grace Elken and Laurence James-Davis are the understudies. The creative team includes designer Anna Fleischle, lighting designer Robbie Butler and casting director Christopher Worrall.

The route includes Bath, Birmingham, Chichester, Glasgow and Dublin, finishing in Coventry on 24th April. Nottingham Playhouse’s announcement also records the production’s Olivier award for Best New Play. A major touring role for a first-time stage performer is a particularly notable aspect of this casting announcement.
