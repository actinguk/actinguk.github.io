Rambert takes It’s a Sin back to the dancefloor

A new trailer offers a first look at the dance adaptation of Russell T Davies’s television drama, ahead of its Manchester premiere.

Acting UK editorial 16th November 2026

A television drama whose characters found freedom on the dancefloor is returning to that setting in a rather literal fashion. A trailer released on 16th November offers a glimpse of Rambert’s adaptation of It’s a Sin, the Channel 4 series about friendship and the AIDS crisis in 1980s London.

Rambert’s artistic director Benoit Swan Pouffer directs and choreographs the production, which begins its tour at Aviva Studios in Manchester on 16th February 2027. Russell T Davies, who created the television series, is an executive producer alongside Pet Shop Boys and Glyn Fussell. The tour continues until 13th November 2027.

The adaptation uses movement, filmed material and narration, with writing by Kaite O’Reilly and music by Roman GianArthur alongside the Pet Shop Boys title song. For performers accustomed to building character through dialogue, the interesting question is how much of a relationship can be communicated through physical proximity, rhythm and the refusal to move.

The production also puts touring at the centre of its plans. Rambert identifies support from Arts Council England’s touring investment pilot, which helps larger theatre and dance productions reach regional audiences. This is a production announcement, not an open casting call.
