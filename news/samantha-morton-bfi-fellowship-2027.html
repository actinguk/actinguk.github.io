Samantha Morton to receive the BFI Fellowship

The BFI announced its highest honour for Morton on 23rd November, with a January presentation and a June retrospective.

Acting UK editorial25th November 2026

The British Film Institute announced on 23rd November that Samantha Morton will receive its Fellowship. The presentation is scheduled for 28th January 2027 at the Rosewood London, during the chair’s dinner hosted by Jay Hunt.

The announcement acknowledges a career that has crossed independent cinema, television and international studio productions without settling into any one of them. Morton’s early work includes Carine Adler’s Under the Skin and Kay Mellor’s Band of Gold; subsequent credits include Morvern Callar, Minority Report and The Walking Dead.

The BFI also singles out her work behind the camera. The Unloved, her drama about a child in the care system, won the television BAFTA for single drama. The institution links the award to her advocacy for access to the arts among children and people from working-class backgrounds.

A retrospective at BFI Southbank is planned for June 2027, including an onstage conversation with Morton. The detailed programme is still to be announced. For now, the Fellowship is recognition of an actor whose career has made room for both unusually intimate independent work and films of considerable scale.
