Sheffield’s Crucible opens Singin’ in the Rain with Grant Almirall

Jonathan Church directs the musical’s Christmas engagement, beginning on 26th November and running to 16th January.

Acting UK editorial26th November 2026

Sheffield’s Crucible begins its festive run of Singin’ in the Rain on 26th November. Jonathan Church directs the production, a collaboration between Sheffield Theatres, Chichester Festival Theatre and Stage Entertainment, in association with Jonathan Church Theatre Productions.

Grant Almirall plays Don Lockwood, with Nicole Lily Baisden as Kathy Selden, Natalie Anderson as Lina Lamont and Matthew Maddison as Cosmo Brown. Wendi Peters plays Dora Bailey. The company includes a substantial dance ensemble, with Andrew Wright leading the choreography and Jayne Elster contributing additional choreography.

The musical makes an unusually entertaining subject of a technological upheaval: the arrival of synchronised sound in Hollywood, which forces silent-film stars to discover whether their voices are quite as marketable as their faces. The production brings that story to a theatre in which the audience can see the performers negotiating its physical demands in real time.

Simon Higlett designs the set and costumes, Tim Mitchell the lighting and Gareth Owen the sound. The run continues until 16th January 2027. This is an opening-day preview based on the theatre’s published production details, not a review.
