The Sorcerer’s Apprentice prepares for its Manchester stage premiere

A ten-strong cast brings Richard Hough and Alexander S Bermange’s musical to HOME from 27th November.

Acting UK editorial22nd November 2026

HOME in Manchester will host the world stage premiere of The Sorcerer’s Apprentice from 27th November 2026 to 2nd January 2027. The new musical has a book and lyrics by Richard Hough, music by Alexander S Bermange and direction by Kirk Jameson. It takes its starting point from Goethe’s poem rather than simply reproducing the animated image many audiences will know from Fantasia.

The story is set in a Scandinavian town threatened with collapse, where a sorcerer and apprentice must attempt to work together. A digital version of the musical appeared in 2021, but the forthcoming production is a developed stage version with additional material and ten performers. That transition from screen-mediated presentation to a live ensemble is a substantial production development, not merely a revival of a familiar title.

The announced cast includes Emma Salvo as Greta, Sophie-Louise Dann as Lamia, Imogen Craig as Eva, Kai Davies as Erik, Mateo Oxley as Fabian and Mark Rice-Oxley as Johan. Jacob Beresford, Adriana Da Cunha, Sharlise Fitzgerald and Josh Rosewood complete the company. Khiley Williams provides choreography and Scott Penrose is magic consultant.

A musical about magic must negotiate a practical paradox: theatrical illusion is strongest when the audience understands, at least partly, that real people are doing the work in front of them. The production’s creative team will decide how spectacle and character share the stage. We cannot judge those choices before opening; the announced run and cast are the news.
