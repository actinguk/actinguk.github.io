Starlight Express rolls into cinemas from Wembley

Brett Sullivan’s film of the London stage revival receives its international cinema release on 26th November.

Acting UK editorial26th November 2026

The filmed edition of Andrew Lloyd Webber’s Starlight Express reaches cinemas on 26th November for a limited engagement. Brett Sullivan directed the screen version, distributed by Trafalgar Releasing, of the revival staged at the Troubadour Wembley Park Theatre.

Jeevan Braich plays Rusty, with Jade Marvin as Momma McCoy, Asher Forth as Electra, Olivia Ringrose as Greaseball and Sophie Naglik as Pearl. Georgia Pemberton and Jaydon Vijn appear as Dinah and Hydra. The stage revival, directed by Luke Sheppard, opened in June 2024.

A show constructed around moving performers and an auditorium adapted for racing presents an interesting problem for a camera. Cinema audiences cannot choose where to look in quite the way that theatre spectators can; the screen director determines the geography and rhythm of the encounter. The release is an opportunity to see how that change of medium works.

The score combines Lloyd Webber’s music and Richard Stilgoe’s lyrics with material introduced for the revival. UK cinema listings confirm the release date. Individual screenings vary by venue; this is a release report, not a review of the recording.
