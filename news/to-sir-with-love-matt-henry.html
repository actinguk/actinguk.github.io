Matt Henry takes the classroom in To Sir, With Love

The Olivier Award-winning performer leads a new musical adaptation touring the UK from April 2027.

Acting UK editorial 16th November 2026

Matt Henry is moving from the fashion office to the classroom. The Olivier Award-winning actor, recently seen in The Devil Wears Prada, will lead To Sir, With Love, a new musical adaptation of E. R. Braithwaite’s memoir. The initial casting was announced on 12th November.

The production opens at Leeds Grand Theatre on 15th April 2027, with a gala on 21st April supporting the Ben Kinsella Trust. It then visits Hull, Manchester and Wimbledon. The tour coincides with the sixtieth anniversary of the film starring Sidney Poitier.

Henry plays Rick Braithwaite, a teacher whose pupils and colleagues challenge his authority and whose own experiences of racism inform his approach to education. The announced cast also includes Amy Di Bartolomeo, Lucy Benjamin, Ben Richards and Georgia May Foote.

The musical follows concert performances at the Gillian Lynne Theatre in 2025. Its score includes the familiar title song by Don Black and Mark London, with new music and lyrics by John Farrar and Kara DioGuardi. For musical theatre performers, the move from concert presentation to a fully staged tour is worth watching: it is a different test of the relationship between a song and the scene that earns it. No open audition has been announced in the sources consulted.
