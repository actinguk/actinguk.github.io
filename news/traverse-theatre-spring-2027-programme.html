Edinburgh’s Traverse announces a spring season built around new writing

Linda McLean’s The Ferry, a Murakami adaptation and Tafadzwa Muchenje’s Ripples feature in the programme announced on 23rd November.

Acting UK editorial24th November 2026

Edinburgh’s Traverse Theatre announced its spring 2027 programme on 23rd November, with work across both stages beginning in January. New Scottish drama sits alongside international collaboration and opportunities for work still in development.

Linda McLean’s The Ferry examines bereavement, family and exploitation associated with county lines drug networks. It is a collaboration with Dundee Rep, Tron Theatre and the English Touring Company, putting several producing organisations behind one new play.

Imogen Stirling’s She Says No begins with a woman seeking shelter after believing she is being followed. Davey Anderson’s Snuff returns in a staging directed by Fraser Scott more than two decades after its debut.

Vanishing Point and Japan’s Kanagawa Arts Theatre collaborate on Confessions of a Shinagawa Monkey, bringing Haruki Murakami’s writing into the programme. Stand and Be Counted presents Ripples by first-time playwright Tafadzwa Muchenje, addressing the treatment of migrants in Britain through a story based on real events.

The programme also retains opportunities for developing work through scratch performances and initiatives including Page2Stage. The pairing of established playwrights with emerging voices is a significant feature of the announcement. Individual casting and performance details can be found through the theatre’s listings.
