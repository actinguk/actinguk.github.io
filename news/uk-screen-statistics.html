The UK screen sector in numbers

The BFI’s latest quarterly summary puts UK cinema box office at £897m for January–September, up 20% year on year.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What this means for actors

Box-office performance is one measure of the screen economy, not a direct measure of acting jobs. Use the BFI's full statistics to distinguish audience revenue from production activity and employment.
