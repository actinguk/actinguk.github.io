WhatsOnStage Awards close nominations ahead of December shortlist

Public nominations for the 27th awards closed at noon on 24th November; the shortlist is due on 10th December.

Acting UK editorial24th November 2026

The first stage of voting for the 27th WhatsOnStage Awards reached its scheduled deadline at midday on 24th November. The organisers plan to announce the shortlist on 10th December, ahead of the ceremony at the London Palladium on 28th February 2027.

The awards distinguish leading and supporting performances in plays and musicals, and recognise two achievements that can disappear in the publicity surrounding new productions: professional stage debuts and performers taking over established roles.

The eligibility rules for the studio category cover venues with fewer than 300 seats, while the regional production award recognises work that began previews outside Greater London. The awards are not simply a survey of the West End, even though the ceremony takes place there.

An independent panel selects technical shortlists, including casting and direction, before readers choose the winners. No nominees have been announced at this stage.

Gina and Mazz Murray are due to host the ceremony for the third consecutive year. The next date to mark is the December shortlist, rather than any prediction about who will take home a trophy.
