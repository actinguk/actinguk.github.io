The Yard makes room for Sondheim

Jay Miller will direct the Hackney Wick theatre’s first musical, Company, ahead of a transfer to Theatr Clwyd and a national tour.

Acting UK editorial 15th November 2026

For its first musical, the Yard Theatre has chosen a work in which the most difficult thing to negotiate is not a dance break but a conversation. Stephen Sondheim and George Furth’s Company will open in Hackney Wick next May, directed by the theatre’s artistic director, Jay Miller.

The production, announced on 13th November, is a collaboration between the Yard and English Touring Theatre, with Theatr Clwyd as an associate partner. It runs in London from 27th May to 10th July 2027, before playing in Mold from 9th to 18th September and embarking on a national tour. Further touring dates are yet to be announced.

Company follows Bobby through a series of encounters with friends and lovers, as the certainties of other people’s relationships make his own indecision increasingly conspicuous. Sondheim’s score is famously demanding, but the dramatic challenge is just as considerable. The performer must make the shifts between conversational awkwardness, musical precision and emotional disclosure feel like parts of the same life.

Màth Roberts is responsible for musical direction, arrangements and orchestrations, with choreography by Malik Nashad Sharpe and casting by Heather Basten. The theatre has not yet announced the cast. That makes this a production to watch rather than an invitation to submit an unsolicited audition: there is no verified open call at present.

Public booking opens at noon on 19th November. For performers interested in musical theatre beyond the West End, the partnership is worth noting. A production originating in an independent London theatre and travelling to a regional stage offers a useful reminder that the musical theatre landscape is rather larger than the streets around Shaftesbury Avenue.
