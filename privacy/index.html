Privacy

How Acting UK uses browser storage and handles links to other websites.

Browser storage

Acting UK uses local browser storage to remember your saved resources and personal career checklist on your device. You can clear these through the homepage’s saved-data control or your browser settings.

External sites

Some pages link to third-party websites or load external images. These services have their own privacy practices; we do not control their processing. GitHub Pages hosts the site.

Questions

For website privacy questions, see contact information. Do not share sensitive information in public issue reports.
