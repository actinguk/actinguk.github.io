Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Agent.co.uk?

Agent.co.uk offers a third-party searchable directory of acting agents. It can help you discover agencies outside the handful of familiar names.

Why it may be useful to actors

Directory search is useful for building an initial shortlist, but third-party entries can become outdated. Cross-check every agency with its own official website and any relevant professional association.

How to use this resource

Make a spreadsheet or save candidates on Acting UK, verify their current roster and submission policy, then approach only those that genuinely fit.

Submission checklist

Check the agency’s actor department, current application instructions and whether it is accepting unsolicited approaches. We have not confirmed an open submission window; do not assume availability from this listing.

Prepare current materials and use the official instructions. Keep the date you checked and the agency response.
