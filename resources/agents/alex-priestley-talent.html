What is Alex Priestley Talent?

Alex Priestley Talent is an agency with a Manchester and London presence. It is worth investigating for actors seeking representation beyond a central London-only shortlist.

Why it may be useful to actors

A regional connection may be useful if you live in the north-west or want to build links across different production centres. Check the roster and the actual locations in which the agency operates.

How to use this resource

Use its current application guidance. State your real base, work permissions and travel availability so the agency can assess practical fit.

Published acting submission instructions

The agency requests a Spotlight link and footage (including self-tapes) by email. It says three weeks without a reply means the application has not progressed and welcomes a later approach with new footage or credits. It asks applicants not to call the office.
