Brennan Artists — representation guide

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Brennan Artists?

Brennan Artists represents Scotland-based actors and creative talent. It is a useful option to investigate if your work and professional network are based in Scotland.

Why it may be useful to actors

A local agency can understand regional production and travel logistics, though representation does not restrict an actor to one region. Compare its roster with your intended stage and screen work.

How to use this resource

Check the official submission route, and be clear about your Scottish base, other locations and availability. Review contract terms before committing.

Published acting submission instructions

Its actors FAQ says applicants should be adults with professional training or experience and a base in Scotland. Send a concise introduction with links to your Spotlight profile or footage rather than large attachments. The agency considers submissions individually, subject to capacity, and asks applicants not to chase.
