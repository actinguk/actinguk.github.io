What is Creative Artists Management?

Creative Artists Management (CAM) is a London actor representation resource. The official site is the best starting point for its current roster and contact information.

Why it may be useful to actors

Compare the kinds of stage and screen work clients undertake and consider whether the agency suits your goals. Do not infer representation services or acceptance criteria beyond what it publishes.

How to use this resource

Read its current submission policy before contacting it. Prepare a concise application with your strongest work and check any contract terms before signing.
