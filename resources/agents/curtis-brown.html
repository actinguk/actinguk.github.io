What is Curtis Brown?

Curtis Brown includes an actors department alongside other creative representation. For actors, the relevant research is the specific department and its agents, rather than the agency's wider literary identity.

Why it may be useful to actors

A broad agency can provide a useful view of different creative industries, but your relationship would be with a particular representative. Study their clients and ask how communication and submissions work.

How to use this resource

Check official representation guidance before applying. Present current work and a specific reason for approaching the relevant agent; respect closed submissions.
