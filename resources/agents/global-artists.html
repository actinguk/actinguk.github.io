Global Artists — representation guide

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Global Artists?

Global Artists represents performers working in film, television and theatre. It is useful to compare with other London agencies when researching a targeted approach.

Why it may be useful to actors

The agency's client list can help you identify the kind of work and performers it represents. Do not confuse an apparent fit with confirmation of availability or an invitation to apply.

How to use this resource

Start with its official contact page and follow any instructions for new talent. If the route is unclear, make a polite enquiry rather than sending large attachments.

Published acting submission instructions

The agency offers an online form and an email route for acting representation. Its email guidance requests a covering letter and a link to your Spotlight CV. It does not accept postal applications and cannot reply to every candidate. The agency represents performers in film, television and theatre.
