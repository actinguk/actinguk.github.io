What is Hamilton Hodell?

Hamilton Hodell represents actors working across stage and screen. It is a useful example of an established agency with a clear actor-focused identity.

Why it may be useful to actors

Research the roster to see whether your experience, casting profile and ambitions complement its clients. Do not assume an established agency is necessarily the best first representative.

How to use this resource

Use the official submission route if available, and send links that open reliably. Read any proposed representation agreement, including commission and exclusivity.

Published acting submission instructions

The agency accepts electronic acting enquiries through its representation address. Identify the individual agent in your subject line and introduction; send digital material, including a reel. It asks applicants not to make follow-up calls. Commercial and voice representation have separate routes.
