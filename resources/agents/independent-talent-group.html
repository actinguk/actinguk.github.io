Independent Talent Group — representation guide

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Independent Talent Group?

Independent Talent Group works across talent and creative representation. Its actor roster is useful for understanding the range of screen and stage careers within a large agency.

Why it may be useful to actors

Look for alignment with your work and the department's remit. A famous client list tells you little about whether an individual agent has capacity or interest in your casting profile.

How to use this resource

Identify the appropriate official submission route and follow it exactly. Avoid sending identical messages to multiple people in the same agency.

Published acting submission instructions

The actors department publishes a dedicated electronic submission route. Include a short covering letter, CV, headshot and reel or work sample, and state whether the approach is for one agent or the whole team. Its guidance gives a four-to-six-week response window and asks applicants not to follow up by telephone. Commercial representation has separate instructions.
