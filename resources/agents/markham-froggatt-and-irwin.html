What is Markham, Froggatt & Irwin?

Markham, Froggatt & Irwin represents actors across film, theatre and television. Its official roster is a useful research tool for performers exploring established agencies.

Why it may be useful to actors

Look for meaningful similarities in the work represented, not simply famous names. Your own casting materials should make your potential contribution clear without pretending to have credits you lack.

How to use this resource

Use published agency contact instructions and keep a record of submissions. Review commission, payment handling and termination terms if offered representation.
