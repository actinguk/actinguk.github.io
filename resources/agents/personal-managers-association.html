What is Personal Managers’ Association?

The Personal Managers’ Association (PMA) is a professional association for talent agents. Its member information and code can help actors research representation.

Why it may be useful to actors

A professional association is useful for checking an agency's stated membership and understanding expected professional conduct. It is not a guarantee of work or a replacement for reading an agreement.

How to use this resource

Use the member directory to build a shortlist, then check each agency's official site and submission status. If you have a contractual concern, seek appropriate advice.
