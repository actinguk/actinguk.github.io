What is The Artists Partnership?

The Artists Partnership represents talent in film, television and theatre. Its official site is the appropriate place to identify the relevant actor representation department.

Why it may be useful to actors

Study the work represented and whether your material demonstrates a genuine fit. Larger talent organisations may have different departments and submission procedures.

How to use this resource

Follow the current official policy, send a short introduction and make your reel and headshot easy to view. Ask about representation terms if invited to a meeting.
