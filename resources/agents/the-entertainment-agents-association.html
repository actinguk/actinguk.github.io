What is The Entertainment Agents Association?

The Entertainment Agents Association offers a directory of member agencies, including agents for actors. It is a useful route to finding regional representation.

Why it may be useful to actors

Its listings can broaden your research beyond the most prominent London agencies. Verify the member's specialism and current services, since entertainment representation can cover different kinds of work.

How to use this resource

Open the relevant member's own website, check its submission guidance and confirm any fees, commission or terms before agreeing to representation.
