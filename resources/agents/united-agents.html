Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is United Agents?

United Agents represents actors across stage and screen as part of a wider talent business. Its actors department is a useful place to study how an established agency presents its clients.

Why it may be useful to actors

Review the individual roster and the kinds of credits clients have rather than assuming that a large agency is a fit for every career stage. Look for a realistic reason an agent might be interested in your work.

How to use this resource

Use the agency's official contact and submission information. Prepare a concise introduction, recognisable headshot, CV and reel; do not infer that books are open from the existence of a contact page.
