What is Backstage UK?

Backstage UK offers casting notices, performer profiles and career guidance. It is useful for actors researching independent opportunities as well as professional work.

Why it may be useful to actors

A broad marketplace can expose you to different project types, but quality and terms vary by notice. Review the role, producer, compensation and usage before applying.

How to use this resource

Check current subscription and application terms on Backstage itself. Verify the production independently, never assume a listing guarantees work, and avoid sending sensitive information without a reason.
