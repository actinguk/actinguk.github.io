What is Casting Callback?

Casting Callback provides casting notices and actor tools; its platform describes applications as free. Check the current terms before relying on that feature.

Why it may be useful to actors

It is an option for self-submitting actors who want to look beyond a single casting service. A free application does not guarantee a booking or remove the need to assess each notice.

How to use this resource

Create accurate materials, apply to relevant projects and verify the casting professional and production. Check compensation, usage, travel and deadlines before accepting.
