What is Casting Collective?

Casting Collective works with supporting artistes for film and television. Background performance can provide experience of a working set and its practical routines.

Why it may be useful to actors

Supporting artiste work is a distinct category from featured acting. It can be valuable paid work and set experience, but does not automatically count as a professional featured acting credit for every purpose.

How to use this resource

Check the current registration terms, availability requirements, pay and travel arrangements. Read the individual production's instructions and do not assume footage will be available for a showreel.
