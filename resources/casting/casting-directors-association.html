What is Casting Directors Association?

The Casting Directors Association provides a directory of casting professionals across commercials, screen and other work. It is particularly useful when researching who casts the projects you want to pursue.

Why it may be useful to actors

Commercial and screen casting can involve different usage, availability and audition considerations. Research the type of projects a professional handles before making contact.

How to use this resource

Use the directory to identify the correct professional and follow their official submission guidance. For a commercial offer, clarify usage, territory, duration and renewals.
