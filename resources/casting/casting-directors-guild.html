What is Casting Directors’ Guild?

The Casting Directors’ Guild is a professional body for casting directors. It is a useful way to learn about the role of casting and find professional industry information.

Why it may be useful to actors

Understanding how casting directors work helps actors approach the industry more realistically. A membership directory should be used for research, not as a mailing list for unsolicited showreels.

How to use this resource

Read the guild's own information and the professional's published contact policy. Follow casting notices and legitimate application routes rather than trying to bypass a brief.
