What is Open Auditions UK?

Open Auditions UK is a resource for finding open auditions, especially where a performer may not need representation to apply.

Why it may be useful to actors

Open calls can help actors enter a new area of work, but notices may close or change. A listing is a lead to investigate, not confirmation of a current opportunity.

How to use this resource

Follow the original producer's application instructions, verify the date and role requirements, and assess travel, pay and safety before attending.
