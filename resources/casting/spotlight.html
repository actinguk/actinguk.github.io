What is Spotlight?

Spotlight is a professional casting platform used by performers, agents and casting professionals in the UK. Its membership has eligibility requirements; see the official joining FAQ for current routes.

Why it may be useful to actors

For eligible performers, a profile can consolidate headshots, credits and showreels and support submissions through the platform. Membership alone does not guarantee auditions, representation or work.

How to use this resource

Check eligibility before paying for training or a subscription. If you do not qualify yet, develop relevant credits and materials through appropriate open opportunities. Compare membership terms and keep your profile current.
