What is StarNow?

StarNow provides casting and creative project listings. Actors can investigate a broad range of opportunities, including those outside traditional agency routes.

Why it may be useful to actors

It may be useful when building initial experience or looking for particular types of performance work. The same caution applies to any open marketplace: verify the project and assess whether it serves your goals.

How to use this resource

Read the current membership and submission terms, and check the producer, compensation and usage. Do not accept unsafe or unclear audition requests.
