What is BFI?

The British Film Institute (BFI) publishes film culture, industry news and research. It is a useful source for understanding the UK screen sector beyond individual casting opportunities.

Why it may be useful to actors

BFI research can add context to claims about production and audience trends. Distinguish official statistics, editorial articles and funding announcements; each serves a different purpose.

How to use this resource

Use the BFI's news and research pages to follow the sector and verify dates. For work opportunities, follow the relevant production and casting channels rather than treating news articles as job adverts.
