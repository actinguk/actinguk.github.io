What is Deadline?

Deadline is an international entertainment trade publication covering film, television and production developments. Its reporting can help UK actors understand the global context of the screen business.

Why it may be useful to actors

International project announcements may reveal broad production trends or UK links, but do not establish an actor's eligibility or the existence of an open call.

How to use this resource

Follow relevant UK-related stories and confirm details with the production's official channels. Treat news as industry intelligence, not a list of jobs.
