What is Screen Daily?

Screen Daily is a film and television industry publication associated with Screen International. It covers production, festivals, finance and distribution as well as industry developments.

Why it may be useful to actors

For screen actors, it is useful for understanding which projects, companies and markets are active. A production announcement is not necessarily an open casting call or permission to contact a producer.

How to use this resource

Follow UK production reporting and research the companies involved, then use legitimate casting routes for applications. Check subscription requirements on the publisher's site.
