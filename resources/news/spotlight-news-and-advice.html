What is Spotlight News & Advice?

Spotlight News & Advice provides career guidance and industry interviews alongside the professional casting service. It can help actors understand how to prepare their materials and navigate auditions.

Why it may be useful to actors

The articles can be particularly useful for profile preparation, self-tapes and learning how different professionals approach casting. Some guidance is platform-specific, so read the context.
