What is The Stage?

The Stage is a UK theatre publication covering news, reviews, interviews and the business of theatre. Its reporting helps performers follow changes that may affect venues, productions and employment.

Why it may be useful to actors

For stage actors, it provides context beyond audition listings: closures, funding, leadership changes and casting announcements can help you understand the market. Some content may require a subscription.

How to use this resource

Read reporting regularly and follow links to the original theatre or producer when a story mentions a casting opportunity. Our casting directory also covers The Stage's jobs service.
