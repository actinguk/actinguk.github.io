What is Variety?

Variety is an international entertainment industry publication covering film, television, business and culture. It is useful for following broader market developments and industry conversations.

Why it may be useful to actors

For UK performers, its coverage can provide context on international productions, distribution and changing business models. It is less targeted to UK open auditions than a local casting platform.

How to use this resource

Read relevant stories, follow the original organisation for project details, and use UK-specific professional resources for contracts and casting applications.
