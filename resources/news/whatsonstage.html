What is WhatsOnStage?

WhatsOnStage covers theatre productions, reviews and casting announcements. It is particularly useful for following the UK theatre scene and understanding which shows are opening or touring.

Why it may be useful to actors

For actors, production announcements and reviews can help identify companies, directors and the kinds of work being produced. Coverage is not a promise of an audition or a complete list of vacancies.

How to use this resource

Follow the official production or venue for dates and any open calls. Use the news as research for your craft and career rather than sending unsolicited messages to a named cast.
