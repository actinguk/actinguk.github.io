Arts Jobs lists opportunities across the creative sector. Actors may find theatre, arts administration and other roles that complement a performance career.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Arts Jobs?

Arts Jobs lists opportunities across the creative sector. Actors may find theatre, arts administration and other roles that complement a performance career.

Why it may be useful to actors

A creative-sector day job can provide income and insight into how organisations work, but the hours must be compatible with your acting commitments. Not all listings are acting auditions.

How to use this resource

Filter by location and role, check the employer's terms and deadline, and compare pay, commute and flexibility before applying.
