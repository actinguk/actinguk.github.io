Creative Access — actor resource guide

Creative Access works to widen access to creative industries through opportunities and support. It is useful for actors exploring related creative work and career development.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Creative Access?

Creative Access works to widen access to creative industries through opportunities and support. It is useful for actors exploring related creative work and career development.

Why it may be useful to actors

Creative-industry roles can offer valuable experience, networks and income between performances. Eligibility and target groups differ by programme, so check the actual opportunity.

How to use this resource

Use the official opportunities and programme pages, and read the criteria before applying. Consider roles that complement your acting goals without assuming they lead directly to casting.
