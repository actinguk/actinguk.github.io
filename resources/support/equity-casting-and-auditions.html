What is Equity: casting and auditions?

Equity's casting and auditions guidance addresses how performers can assess casting requests and work within reasonable professional boundaries.

Why it may be useful to actors

It is particularly useful before a self-tape, unfamiliar audition or request involving intimate content. The union's guidance can help distinguish a reasonable process from something that needs clarification.

How to use this resource

Read the relevant guidance and contact Equity for advice where appropriate. Verify the production, casting professional, venue, terms and any sensitive requests independently.
