What is Equity?

Equity is the UK trade union for performers and other entertainment professionals. It offers resources on contracts, working conditions, pay, auditions and industry campaigns.

Why it may be useful to actors

For actors who work freelance or across multiple employers, union guidance can help you evaluate agreements and know where to seek support. Applicable rates depend on the engagement and agreement.

How to use this resource

Check current membership eligibility and access to advice. Before accepting a contract, review pay, usage, cancellation and any AI or replica clauses; seek individual advice when necessary.
