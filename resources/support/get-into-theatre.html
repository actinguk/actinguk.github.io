What is Get Into Theatre?

Get Into Theatre provides information on theatre careers, training and opportunities. It is relevant to actors who want to understand both performance and the wider teams that make theatre possible.

Why it may be useful to actors

Exploring adjacent roles can help you develop a sustainable career and understand how productions operate. The site is especially useful for early-career research and discovering routes you might not know.

How to use this resource

Browse the specific career or opportunity category, then check the original provider's current dates, eligibility and application information.
