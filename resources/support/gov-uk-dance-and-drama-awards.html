Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Gov.uk: Dance and Drama Awards?

The UK government's Dance and Drama Awards scheme provides funding support for eligible training at participating institutions. Eligibility depends on the scheme's current rules, course and individual circumstances.

Why it may be useful to actors

For someone comparing vocational training, a funding route can materially change affordability. Do not assume every drama school or acting course participates, or that an award covers all costs.

How to use this resource

Check the official eligibility and provider list before paying a deposit. Ask the school how awards, tuition and living-cost support apply to your own offer.
