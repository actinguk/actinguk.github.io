HMRC: Self Assessment — actor resource guide

HMRC's Self Assessment guidance explains UK tax return obligations. Actors who earn freelance income may need to understand registration, record-keeping and reporting requirements.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is HMRC: Self Assessment?

HMRC's Self Assessment guidance explains UK tax return obligations. Actors who earn freelance income may need to understand registration, record-keeping and reporting requirements.

Why it may be useful to actors

Keeping clear records of invoices, income and expenses can reduce stress between jobs and help you assess what you actually earn. Employment status and tax treatment can vary between engagements.

How to use this resource

Read HMRC's guidance for your situation, check current deadlines and seek qualified tax advice if unsure. Do not treat all payments or expenses as having the same tax treatment.
