Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Into Film?

Into Film supports film education and youth filmmaking. It can be useful to early-career performers who want to understand storytelling and how films are made.

Why it may be useful to actors

Experience of filmmaking can improve an actor's understanding of camera coverage, collaboration and editing. The organisation's activities have specific age and programme criteria.

How to use this resource

Explore current opportunities and resources, verify eligibility and permissions, and look separately for professional casting notices if you are seeking paid acting work.
