What is Prospects?

Prospects offers a career profile for actors as part of its graduate careers information. It can be a useful perspective for students comparing acting with other employment routes.

Why it may be useful to actors

Its occupational overview helps distinguish the day-to-day realities of freelance performance from the idealised picture of an acting career. Use it alongside practical union and casting advice.

How to use this resource

Read the skills and employment sections, then build a plan for training, experience, income and professional materials that matches your circumstances.
