Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is ScreenSkills?

ScreenSkills supports skills and training across the UK screen industries. Actors can use it to understand production roles and the broader film and television environment.

Why it may be useful to actors

Knowing how departments work can make you a better collaborator on set and open up adjacent professional skills. Not every ScreenSkills training opportunity is intended for actors.

How to use this resource

Search the relevant screen sector and check each programme's audience and eligibility. Compare training with your own goals rather than applying to everything.
