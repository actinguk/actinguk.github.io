What is Shape Arts?

Shape Arts is a disability-led arts organisation focused on access and creative development. It is relevant to performers looking for accessible artistic opportunities and sector support.

Why it may be useful to actors

Its work can help actors think about access as a practical part of training, auditions and employment rather than an afterthought. Programmes and funding opportunities vary.

How to use this resource

Review current projects and eligibility directly. Ask training providers and productions about the adjustments you need, and use appropriate specialist advice for individual situations.
