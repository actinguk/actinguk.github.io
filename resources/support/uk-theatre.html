Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is UK Theatre?

UK Theatre is an industry body supporting theatre organisations and the sector. Its resources can help actors understand the business context behind productions and employment.

Why it may be useful to actors

Knowing how theatre employers operate is useful when reading industry news or considering work beyond performing. The organisation is not a general casting service for actors.

How to use this resource

Use its official resources for sector information and check Equity for performer-specific contract advice. Follow individual theatres for actual auditions and vacancies.
