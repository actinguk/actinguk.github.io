What is ArtsEd?

ArtsEd offers acting and musical theatre education in London. For actors deciding between screen, stage and musical work, its separate disciplines make it useful to compare training pathways.

Why it may be useful to actors

Choose the course by its curriculum, not by its association with musical theatre or the success of individual alumni. Ask about screen projects, live performance, showcase format and access support.

How to use this resource

Check audition instructions and tuition for your chosen programme. Prepare only the material requested and budget for travel and the full course duration.
