What is East 15 Acting School?

East 15 Acting School is part of the University of Essex. Its theatre and acting provision includes different approaches and study locations, so check the campus attached to the exact programme.

Why it may be useful to actors

It can be a useful comparison for applicants interested in practical training outside central London or specialist performance routes. A course title alone does not establish which campus, techniques or production experiences you will have.
