The Federation of Drama Schools is a starting point for comparing member institutions and professional drama training in the UK.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Federation of Drama Schools?

The Federation of Drama Schools is a starting point for comparing member institutions and professional drama training in the UK.

Why it may be useful to actors

Use the member directory to discover schools you might otherwise overlook. Membership is a useful research signal, not proof that every course is suitable for you or that a graduate will get work.

How to use this resource

Follow links to individual schools and compare course content, qualifications, audition fees, student support and funding. Keep a shortlist based on course fit rather than rankings.
