Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Guildford School of Acting?

Guildford School of Acting is part of the University of Surrey. It offers acting and musical theatre training in Guildford, rather than requiring a London base.

Why it may be useful to actors

The university setting and Surrey location can suit performers who want a campus environment. Assess whether the acting or musical theatre course aligns with your strengths and target work.

How to use this resource

Check auditions, course structure and accommodation costs through GSA and the university. Factor in rail travel if you plan to attend London industry events.
