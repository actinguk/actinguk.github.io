Guildhall School of Music & Drama — actor training guide

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Guildhall School of Music & Drama?

Guildhall School of Music & Drama is based at the Barbican in London and brings drama and music training into the same institution. It is a useful option for applicants interested in intensive performance training within a wider performing-arts community.

Why it may be useful to actors

The location and multidisciplinary environment may appeal to actors who want exposure to music and theatre-making. Compare actual acting curriculum, performance projects and teaching commitments with other London conservatoires.

How to use this resource

Use the drama department's official admissions information for audition stages and current dates. Ask about living costs, access requirements and the practical workload before accepting an offer.
