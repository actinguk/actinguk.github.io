What is Identity School of Acting?

Identity School of Acting offers part-time training and operates in several locations and online. It is worth considering if a full-time conservatoire course is impractical.

Why it may be useful to actors

Part-time training can be combined with work and regular audition practice, but requires you to build your own experience and professional materials. Check the actual schedule and level of each class.

How to use this resource

Ask about entry assessment, attendance, total fees and progression. Do not assume a part-time course qualifies for Spotlight or immigration routes; verify separately.
