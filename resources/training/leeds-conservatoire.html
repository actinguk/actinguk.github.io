Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is Leeds Conservatoire?

Leeds Conservatoire offers performance-related training in a major Yorkshire city. Its theatre and performance course options should be checked individually before applying.

Why it may be useful to actors

It may suit applicants wanting a creative city outside London and an environment that connects different performance disciplines. Check whether the named course is straight acting, musical theatre or another specialism.

How to use this resource

Use the current course list for eligibility, award and application requirements. Compare the specific practical curriculum rather than treating all conservatoire courses as interchangeable.
