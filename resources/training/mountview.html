What is Mountview?

Mountview in Peckham offers acting and musical theatre training. It is worth investigating if you want to study performance in south London and choose between straight acting and musical theatre pathways.

Why it may be useful to actors

The distinction matters: musical theatre may require singing and dance preparation in addition to acting. Read the exact course modules and audition requirements before paying application fees.

How to use this resource

Compare full-time commitments and travel to Peckham with your other options. Check current admissions deadlines and funding directly on Mountview's site.
