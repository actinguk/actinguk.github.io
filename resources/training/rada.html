RADA — actor training guide

Royal Academy of Dramatic Art (RADA) offers intensive actor training in central London. Its three-year BA Acting includes voice, movement, text, screen and audio work; shorter courses and a non-accredited foundation route serve different goals.

Acting UK editorialPublished 13 November 2026

What is RADA?

Royal Academy of Dramatic Art (RADA) offers intensive actor training in central London. Its three-year BA Acting includes voice, movement, text, screen and audio work; shorter courses and a non-accredited foundation route serve different goals.

Why it may be useful to actors

RADA’s BA admissions are audition-based and the course has a substantial in-person workload. Compare the BA with the foundation rather than assuming that the latter leads automatically into the degree. The foundation is non-award-bearing and does not attract student finance; check current course fees and funding on the official pages.

How to use this resource

Start with the BA course and its audition guidance, then compare short workshops if you are testing your commitment. Budget for central London travel, accommodation and time unavailable for other employment.
