What is Rose Bruford College?

Rose Bruford College in Sidcup offers acting and theatre training alongside other performing-arts disciplines. It is particularly relevant to applicants who want to explore the wider theatre-making environment.

Why it may be useful to actors

A location outside central London can change travel and housing calculations, but does not automatically make living costs low. Compare course specialism, ensemble work and final-year performance opportunities.
