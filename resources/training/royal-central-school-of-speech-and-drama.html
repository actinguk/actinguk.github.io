What is Royal Central School of Speech and Drama?

Royal Central School of Speech and Drama offers acting and a range of specialist theatre and performance disciplines. Applicants should distinguish its different course routes instead of treating the institution as one uniform acting programme.

Why it may be useful to actors

The range of theatre-making disciplines can be especially relevant if you are interested in collaboration, devised work or developing alongside directors and designers. Check the named course's emphasis, not just the school name.

How to use this resource

Read the programme specification and application guidance for the precise course you want. Compare practical work, assessment, industry exposure and funding with other offers.
