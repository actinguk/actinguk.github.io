What is Royal Conservatoire of Scotland?

Royal Conservatoire of Scotland in Glasgow offers BA Acting with stage, screen and audio training. It is a relevant choice for performers looking at Scottish and UK-wide creative careers.

Why it may be useful to actors

Training in Glasgow may suit someone who wants a major production and theatre city outside London. Compare the course's actual ensemble, camera and voice work against your goals.
